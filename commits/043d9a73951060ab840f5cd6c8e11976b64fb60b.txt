D156: the wide mission's world from the game's state, at any viewport
The owner's direction: a widescreen mission shows everything, at any width and
height, with no fake. The wide picture was the VDP's 320x224 window plus a band
of sprites appended after BuildSpriteTable and plane cells rewritten in the
cartridge's V-RAM, capped at the sprite field's 127 columns; the band lost the
cursor and the selection box (screen and non-unit nodes were never appended),
the effects (records outside UnitArray) and every frame another build site made,
drew the units' facing from the record's +$66 instead of the +$68 the builder
reads, and could not grow past 447 columns or 240 lines.

next/mission_world.c reads the world from the cartridge's memory -- MapCells and
the art table data_059FBE (with DrawMapCellTile's $FFFFC195 void switch), and
every node of the mission pool with the game's own rules for whether it is drawn
(the hidden bit, the blink read back from the state the build left), its
position (world or screen node, the facing step at +$68) and its parts' tile
words -- and the renderer draws it for the viewport. The append and the band
fill are gone, so the two cursor rows and the map view are the cartridge's
instructions again and nothing writes the cartridge's sprite table or V-RAM.

The viewport is the target over the world scale, both ways, up to the largest
map (2048): the world scale is the player's wish (no 447-column raise, no
cover-the-height raise) and the HUD is its own layer at the interface's scale.
The bounds follow it: the camera's left limit +extra, the cursor's record clamp
-extra (it reaches the viewport, D147's open question), the vertical words by
the signed lines the viewport shows or cuts (the border is the world's now), a
window wider than the map closed on its centre, and a bound never moved past
the camera (the edge scroll hands such an overflow to the cursor unclamped --
measured: the record thrown to -3 102 in eleven frames). The pointer reaches the
viewport's lines.

tests/test_mission_world.sh measures it against the cartridge's own picture on
stretches of the Atreides mission and battle at 854x480 and at 2560x1440 (AUTO,
3x, 2x): the cartridge's window pixel for pixel, the timing differences named,
and every part beyond the window the one the cartridge's own BuildSpriteTable
draws there with its camera moved (run by the interpreter on the frame's RAM),
the cursor and the selection box included; the VDP's own band and a dropped part
are its controls. The fixtures whose numbers were the VDP picture's (the 447
cap, D145's height raise and HUD scale, ticket 152's +extra record clamp, the
unsigned cut lines) are edited with that reason.
