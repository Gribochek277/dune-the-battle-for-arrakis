ticket 100: the resource format, read out of LoadScreenResource rather than inferred
An entry's non-zero longs are pointers whose low byte selects a destination record
from the table at $022608 and whose address holds a list of {length word, source
long} pairs ended by a zero word, bit 15 of the length word taking a different
branch; the entry's first long goes to UploadGraphicBlocks and the routine ends with
FlushDmaQueue. Entry $0E is graphics from $1032A0 and a second upload from
$053234.

The data is uncompressed -- VRAM $0000 matches ROM $1032A0 byte for byte for at
least 16 KiB -- so the port's loader is a copy, not a decoder, which is the fact that
made this ticket worth starting with the measurement instead of with the code.

Three things stay open and are named as open: the length word's unit ($02D2 is 722
and 16 KiB matched, so it is not bytes as they stand), what the destination records
hold beyond the word read into d4, and what bit 15 selects. Guessing any of them
produces a loader that fills this screen correctly and the next one wrongly, which is
the failure mode worth the extra hour.
