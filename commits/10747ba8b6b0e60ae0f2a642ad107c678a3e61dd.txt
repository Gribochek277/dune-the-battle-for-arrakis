the house selection's control is a perturbed run, because ticket 228 removed the drift it caught
The check's negative control ran the identical pair through a Z80 counter bound
of 1 and required a refusal: the drift used to be 128 cycles, so a bound of 1
had to fail. Ticket 228 made the seam advance the sound and the Z80 once per
dispatch and made every row record its guest's instructions, so the same pair's
counters are now equal (+0) and a bound of 1 correctly accepts them -- the
control was asserting the bug it had been written to catch.

The control is now built the other way round: a deliberately perturbed next run
must be rejected at the same bound of 128 the identical pair passes, and the
refusal has to say what differed. That still fails if --z80-counter-drift waives
everything, and it also fails if the comparator stops reading the counters at
all.
