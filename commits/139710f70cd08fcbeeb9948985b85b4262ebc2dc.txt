Audio: the window plays the samples the harness measures
The renderer's buffer was a measurement sink, so the music ticket 68 synthesised
had nowhere to go: the window opened no audio device at all. It now installs a
streaming sink -- md68k_sound_set_sink() hands a front end every sample as it is
made, fed by the same branch of the same sample window that appends to the
recording -- and port/runtime/audio.c is the sink it installs: a single-producer/
single-consumer ring SDL's audio callback pulls from, with the latency the device
starts on (three guest frames, 2 205 samples, 50 ms), a 65 536-sample ring, and
the two clocks' difference turned into underrun/overrun counters instead of
noise (an overrun skips the oldest audio on the consumer's side, so the producer
never waits).

The device is asked for the renderer's own format exactly (44 100 Hz, mono,
16-bit, 1024 samples per callback) with allowed_changes off, so the buffer the
callback receives is the stream the machine made; a device that will not take it
is SDL's to convert, after the callback. A machine with no sound device, or
--no-audio, prints why and plays silent. --record-audio PATH streams what the
sink produced to a WAV (wav.c grew begin/append/finish for it).

The claim is checked two ways. port/tests/test_audio.c renders a second through
the machine's own chip with the recorder *and* the sink on and compares all
44 100 samples one by one with md68k_sound_samples(), plus the ring's
push/pull/wrap-around, its underrun and overrun policies and the latency gate;
tests/test_play_audio.sh runs the window against SDL's dummy video and audio
drivers and requires its --record-audio WAV to be byte-identical to
port/build/headless-fast --wav for the same 120 frames (94 937 samples, peak
438), a paced run to report zero underruns and zero overruns with
produced = played + buffered, the guest's rate to stay in ticket 61's band with
the audio on (600 frames: 59.93 with the device against 59.93 with --no-audio,
the audio path costing 0.9 ms of the 16.7 ms frame), and a missing device to
degrade to silence and record the same audio anyway.

tests/test_pacing.sh and tests/test_recording.sh now pin SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy
for the same reason they pin the video driver: the host's sound server is not
what they are measuring.
