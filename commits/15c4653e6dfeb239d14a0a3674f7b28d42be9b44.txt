The clock's itemisation is a check, and the credit roll's ledger moves with it
tests/test_clock_budget.sh pins what the charges are worth at the run level: the
machine's counter is exactly the sum of the buckets the state dump reports, the
Z80's window is 8 cycles an access and the Z80 area 1, the VBlank entry is 34 a
frame on every entry, and the mission recording's VDP busy windows are printed
so the DMA the port charges through the guest's poll loop stays visible. The
control is --no-contention: every one of ticket 115's charges goes to zero, the
bucket arithmetic still holds, and the access counts are still reported, which is
what makes the itemisation a measurement rather than an arithmetic exercise.

tests/test_mission_credits.sh accepts the measured state instead of ticket 113's
one-tick-ahead roll: the port holds 986 + 0/8 at mission clock 210 (two entries
over the reference's 208) and draws 986, the reference's digit, so the check now
requires the drawn value to be the reference's in both accepted pairs -- the
reference's own 985 + 4/8 at 208, which a future ticket that closes the lead
would produce, and this one. The --no-refresh control (216, 986 + 5/8) is joined
by --no-contention (214, 986 + 4/8, drawn 987); neither satisfies the predicate.

tests/run_all.sh registers the new check and its comment blocks carry the new
measurement.
