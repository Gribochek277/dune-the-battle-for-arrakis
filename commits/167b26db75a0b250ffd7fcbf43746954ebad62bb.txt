Fidelity: the briefing text is the cartridge's font, and it has its own gate
Ticket 64's report was that the briefing screen draws the glyphs with the
bottom row missing and holes inside the letters ("ARE", "BEING",
"PEACEABLE"). Those words are the mentat's house briefing, the three pages he
reads after the house selection (entries 2920..3510, 3520..4000, 4010..4280),
and the measurement says the port draws them exactly as the reference does:

  * entry 3500 (page one, "HOUSE ATREIDES HAS A LONG TRADITION OF BEING FAIR
    AND JUST ADMINISTRATORS."): 0 of 71 680 active-area pixels differ, measured
    through two capture routes (scripts/replay-recording.py --checkpoint
    briefing-text-page, 68.8 s, and the dashboard's own shared session);
  * entry 3700 (page two, "OUR PEOPLE ARE LOYAL, HARD WORKING, AND
    PEACEABLE."): 490 pixels differ, all of them the mentat's portrait
    (x 33..76 y 145..181), none in the text rows;
  * entry 5398 (the briefing-text checkpoint): 67 pixels differ, all of them
    the portrait's eye (x 37..76 y 132..138).

The 67 pixels, in the VDP's own terms: at the first one (39, 132) both planes
contribute tile $000 (V-RAM $E988 and $C988), so neither plane draws it; the
sprite attribute table at $C000 puts it on entry #9 (y 112, x 32, 3x3 tiles
from base $589, palette 3, priority), row 3 of tile $58A at V-RAM $B14F = $A9
-- colour index 9, CRAM palette 3 entry $46A = (174,119,87) -- where the
reference's picture has index 10, entry $248 = (146,87,49): the same palette,
one index apart, inside the iris. The port's own frames 5380..5420 are the
same picture in that region (0 changed pixels), so the two sides hold
different portrait tiles at this entry: the animation's step, not the glyphs
and not the renderer.

Why the glyphs look clipped is the cartridge: the font is a white glyph with a
one-pixel black drop shadow baked into the tile, and the briefing screen draws
it with palette 2, where entry 13 is white and entry 15 is black -- so the
eighth row (and the shadow inside the strokes) is black on a black background
on both sides. V-RAM holds the cartridge's own glyph table unchanged (letter
'A' at ROM $057456, 'H' at $057536, 'T' at $0576B6, i.e. $057436 + (letter -
'A' + 1) * 32), and the port's tiles at entry 3500 match those bytes exactly.

So the ticket leaves behind a gate instead of a renderer change:
analysis/checkpoints.json gains briefing-text-page (3500, pixel-exact, 0
threshold), the frame the report is about, and tests/test_fidelity.sh now
requires it to stay at 0 pixels and derives its checkpoint counts from the
store so the catalog can grow. docs/native-port.md records the measurement
(the pages table, the sprite-level evidence, the font bytes) under "The
briefing text (ticket 64)".

bash tests/run_all.sh ends with ALL CHECKS PASSED (25 pass), the baseline tag
still rebuilds byte-identically, and no game source changed.
