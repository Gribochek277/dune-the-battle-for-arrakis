ticket 136: wide backdrop art for the full-screen screens
The build panel, OPTIONS and the house selection keep their 4:3 picture and
their panels, text and cursor at the same pixels; a wide target's bars now hold
a port art asset instead of black. A 4:3 target has no bar and is unchanged.

- next/wide_backdrop.c draws the three screens' art in their own palettes and
  recognises the screens from their measured VDP layouts (six other catalogued
  screens are the negative controls), so the feature runs in either engine.
- app/backdrop.c composes it after the renderer and before the pointer's
  overlay, through the picture region's transform, and feathers the picture's
  edge into the art; the layout, the scene, the seam and the backends are
  unchanged.
- vdp-render composes the same picture (--no-backdrop is the control).
- Docs: widescreen, display, next-engine, port-architecture (README split at
  the 64 KiB bound), the reverse-engineering measurement and D63.
- Tests: port/tests/test_wide_backdrop.c (in make test),
  tests/test_wide_backdrop.sh (fast) and
  tests/test_wide_backdrop_window.sh (slow); the mission-wide check's
  black-bar assertion is kept with the feature removed as the control.
