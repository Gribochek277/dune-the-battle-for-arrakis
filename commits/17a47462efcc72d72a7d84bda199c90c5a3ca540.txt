docs: the work-RAM map and what it measured (M2-2)
The understanding pass said it needed the instrument that measures executions;
this is its result. docs/reverse-engineering-notes.md gains 'M2-2', which
records the run (reset to the third VBlank entry, 107162 instructions, 43217
work-RAM accesses from 211 pcs), the two blanket RAM initialisations that make
the never-touched list empty, the capture cross-check (165 bytes change between
frames 1 and 3 and all 165 are written in the same window), and the structures
the measurement established with their ranges, widths, initialisers and
readers: the frame state block, the DMA queue entry and its pointers, the sound
queue's drain, the countdown pair, the decompressor's 4 KiB destination, a
208-node linked list nothing has read yet, and the stack.

It also states what the measurement cannot see: the record arrays M2-1 named
(UnitArray, StructureArray, SideRecords, MapCells) lie entirely in the bytes
only the initialisations touch, so their layout is not measurable until a run
reaches gameplay -- which needs the VDP work, because the port stops in the
DMA-busy wait at $000E68.

docs/build-and-test.md describes the new gate check and its cost, port/README.md
and docs/native-port.md the harness option, README.md the derived analysis
output.
