Unit icon: the tile is the unit's facing angle, measured on both sides (ticket 109)
The mission's sprite-frame selector sub_00B3B2 reads the current-angle byte
of a unit's angle triple at +8 and writes the descriptor it selects from
data_08424A into the sprite node, so the icon's tile word is a function of
one byte of unit state.  At entry 7898 the unit that draws sprite entry 10
(record 33, $FF120C, byte $FF1276) holds $C0 on the port (descriptor 247,
$0E7BDC, tile $3E6, H flip) and $68 on the reference (descriptor 244,
$0E7BB2, tile $3DA, V flip), and neither side advances it over entries
7890..7910 -- so the port is neither one step ahead nor one step behind.

The two runs part between entries 7680 and 7700: the reference's unit is
mid-turn (step $F8, target $68, current $B0) while the port's is not
($00/$C0/$C0), with the order pointer, the order id and the position still
equal.  The port's unit never turns; that missed turn is the open finding.

The other three known-bad entries do not share this cause: the briefing
portrait does not advance over its window either (in step today), and the
build panel's bar is time-driven on the port while the reference's pictures
at 9497..9499 are identical.
