next: the edge band is a position, and a resting hand keeps the camera moving
Ticket 143 gave the pointer its own band at the picture's edge but still
decided a pointer frame by the pointer's *motion*. A hand pushed to the edge got
one small scroll step, the cursor's record was pulled to the band's inner edge
(304 of 320), and every later frame found the record inside the band: nothing
moved until the mouse moved again. A PC RTS scrolls for as long as the hand
rests at the edge, so the band's membership is a **position** now (D63).

* `next/mission_pointer.{c,h}`: the position is kept for every poll, moved or
  not, and `next_mission_pointer_valid()` says whether this frame's poll was a
  point on the picture at all. `moved`/`clicked` stay what they were -- the
  edges the device latch reads -- so a resting hand is not an edge and cannot
  take a frame away from a held d-pad.
* `next/mission_edge_band.{c,h}`: the per-frame call takes the position, keeps
  the D58 latch, answers `next_mission_edge_band_holds_position()` (the frame is
  the pointer's, the hand is on the picture, inside the band, and the frame is
  not one of its edges'), and on a pointer frame with no position gives the
  sixteen band instructions a band that contains every value: a hand nobody has
  seen is in no band, never a default of (0,0) that would put it in the left
  one. The sixteen instructions, their operands and their per-address charges
  are unchanged.
* `next/mission_cursor.{c,h}`: `next_mission_cursor_hold_pointer()` writes the
  hand's pixel into `$FFFFBF12`/`$FFFFBF14` and forgets the coast
  `$FFFFBF48` on the frames the band holds, called from `$003118`/`$003AD0`
  where the device has just been decided. The guest's own distance-outside-the-
  band arithmetic then sees the same distance every frame and takes the same
  step -- floor one pixel, remainder `$FFFFBF3C`/`$FFFFBF3E` zero, the map's
  edge clamp holding with nothing carried -- instead of a distance that decays
  as the record converges. The move's entry write, the click's "no write", and
  the middle drag's "the view moves under the cursor" are untouched.
* `port/tests/test_replacement.c`: the pointer-band fixture's resting run (24
  frames, the camera one pixel a frame), and a new one comparing the same
  distance outside the pointer's band and the pad's (the same one-pixel step),
  the pad's route of the same length (a step on every frame), the hand leaving
  the band (stopped on that frame), a pointer frame with no position (nothing),
  and the map's edge (held, remainder zero).
* `tests/test_mission_edge_band.sh`: nine window runs on the Atreides recording.
  A hand resting at 310 holds the record on its pixel and takes the camera from
  657 at entry 7 700 to 757 at 7 800; a hand moved out at 7 860 leaves it on 817
  at both 7 880 and 7 960; the middle and switch runs are ticket 143's numbers
  (the camera added as the number the picture moves by); the control build with
  `next/mission_edge_band.c` stubbed puts the same pointer back on the guest's
  200 with the camera 30 pixels along.
* `docs/{mouse,next-engine,port-architecture}.md`, `docs/decisions.md` (D63) and
  the rebuilt site say the band is a position, with the measurements.

`legacy` never sets a pointer and is untouched: `tests/test_mission_cursor.sh`
(the pad's transcribed differential) and `tests/test_mission_mouse.sh` (idle and
`--no-mouse` write the same 64 KiB) are unedited and green.
