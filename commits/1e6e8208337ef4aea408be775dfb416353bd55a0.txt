port: translate the remaining opcodes, and fix what the two paths disagreed on
Ticket 45. The fallback histogram named the gap: btst/bset/bclr/bchg, movea,
movem, the shift/rotate family, neg/negx, ext, swap, dbf and the long tail
(adda/suba/cmpa, mulu/muls/divu/divs, not, nbcd, abcd/sbcd, exg, link/unlk,
eor, Scc, CHK, TRAP/STOP/RTE/RTR, the group-4 SR/CCR/USP moves, jsr/jmp through
every control effective address, and the (d8,PC,Xn) LEA). The translated share
goes from 80.6 % to 99.962 % (39 578 of 39 593); the 15 left are invalid
encodings that reach the interpreter through MD68K_FALLBACK (13 subq.b + 1
addq.b with an address-register destination, 1 bchg d5,#$FF).

The emitters use shared read-modify-write and address helpers so (An)+/-(An)
steps exactly once, and the shift/rotate and BCD cores moved into the runtime
as md68k_shift()/md68k_bcd() so a translated and an interpreted instruction
cannot disagree about the awkward flags.

Ticket 44's dual-path method exposed real disagreements in code that had been
written long before. Both sides are fixed: the interpreter had SUB and CMP
comparing their operands the wrong way round, MOVEA.W/L <ea>,A7 rejected as
illegal (the boot code sets its own stack pointer with it), CLR and Scc using
the read-modify-write store helper although they only write, the immediate
group/ALU-to-memory/ADDQ/SUBQ stepping (An)+/-(An) twice, ADDQ/SUBQ #n,An
setting the condition codes, and ABCD/SBCD moving A7 by one for a byte operand.
The emitters gained the size-preserving Dn store ticket 44 documented, operand
reads sequenced before the registers they share with the operation, and real
MD68K_FALLBACK cases where a form is not translated instead of empty cases that
fell off the end of md68k_translated_step().

port/generated/game.c is regenerated with the merged transpiler.
