scene: the mission's world as a layer of the scene, drawn by both backends at any viewport (D156)
A scene state carries a world beside the VDP's picture (md_scene_world): the
map's cell words and the art table they index, every part the game draws with
no cull and no per-line or per-table limit, and the camera. When a state's
world is active the world pass draws it instead of the VDP's planes and
sprites -- for every row and column of the viewport, the VDP's border lines
included -- with the reference's priority comparison, and leaves the
interface's sprites to the interface layer as before.

The world comes from a source the app hands the renderer
(md_renderer_set_world_source()); the scene fills it after each capture
(md_scene_fill_world()) and calls a world that appears or goes a cut. The
picture has a vertical source too (`source_y`): a viewport taller than the
cartridge's 240 lines shows rows above and below the frame, and the scene
draws up to MD_SCENE_MAX_WIDTH x MD_SCENE_MAX_HEIGHT (2048, the largest map).

Vulkan draws the same world in its first pass (the cells, the table, the parts
and per-band part masks in the state's buffer) and grows the pictures' buffer
to the tallest viewport asked for; scene-vk-test holds it to the CPU backend's
bytes on a viewport larger than the frame both ways, thirty parts on a band, an
interface part, the blend's two worlds and the switch back to the VDP.
