163: the widescreen band is the map's own cells
The wide picture's band (the `extra` guest columns left of the guest's
320-column window, tickets 132/159) is the VDP plane's cells at negative source
x, and the guest's own redraw never writes them: it only places the cells its
320-pixel picture shows. Before this the band held whatever the plane had when
the view last passed. The measurement on the Atreides recording at 16:9 (Right
held from entry 7 700): at entry 7 705 the band's visible plane columns 3..16
held 756 zero words of 812 -- the first at column 3 (guest x -111), row 0,
plane B: $0000 where the map's tile is $0213; a misplaced tile appeared by
entry 7 870 (column 52, 66 words), and by 7 900's scroll the band's cells at
the plane's right end were wrong too (columns 60..63, 96 words).

The fill is the port's C, in the two places the ticket names:
next_mission_wide_band_columns() and next_mission_wide_band_word()
(next/mission_wide.c) name the band's plane columns and the map's tile for
each, with the guest's own DrawMapCellTile ($002FEC) arithmetic and MapCells'
tile word through the cartridge's table at data_059FBE;
next_mission_map_view_band_fill() (next/mission_map_view_plane.c) writes both
planes' words once a guest frame at the frame boundary (app/run_play.c calls it
after the entry's state is taken and before the VBlank handler writes the
hscroll table from the same work-RAM words). V-RAM is written directly: the
guest's VDP counters, its clock and its RAM stay the guest's, and at 0 extra
the fill writes nothing (the 4:3 path is the cartridge's). No replacement row
is added and the guest's 320-wide routines are unchanged.

tests/test_mission_band.sh (slow tier): states saved at entries 7 700 and 7 800
of the held-right run, stepped by the window under Right, Down and both,
eighteen samples at 16:9 and 21:9 plus every frame of a 24-entry moving
stretch. Each frame's dump is compared with the same state fully repainted by
the port's own fill (mission-wide-test --fill-band), and every visible band
cell with the map's own tile (scripts/compare_band.py, no port code), with a
control that empties a cell and requires the comparison to report it. The 16:9
guest part is the same frame's 4:3 picture and the 21:9 one its 3x crop;
--fill-band 0 is a no-op. port/tests/test_mission_wide.c pins the columns and
the words (16:9's 107 and 21:9's 53, the vertical scroll, the map's edge).
