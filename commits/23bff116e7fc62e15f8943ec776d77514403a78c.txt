world: the HUD's screen nodes are the interface's; vdp-render draws the world (D156)
The mission pool's screen nodes are the HUD and the cursor's bracket. The HUD
nodes are the credits and icons, the radar frame $FFFFBF76 and its marker
$FFFFBF4C. The bracket's record is the view record $FFFFBF12. Before this
change the world layer drew every screen node at its cartridge screen place,
so at a HUD scale different from the world's the HUD's sprites appeared twice.

next_mission_world_build() now marks every screen node except the bracket as
the HUD's (md_scene_world's new hud[]). Both backends then leave those parts
to the interface layer whenever the layout has one; the CPU backend's
world_sprite_line and Vulkan's pack_world use the same rule. A
mission-world fixture checks the mark.

vdp-render gains --world RAM [--world-rom PATH] [--world-parts off]. It reads
the world from a work-RAM dump of the same entry and draws it for the wide
layout's viewport, as the window does from the machine. The band and wide
gates draw their pictures with it.
