194: the graphics are files, and the files are the cartridge's bytes
The cartridge's art is out of the ROM as one editable text file per region
under `assets/` -- 102 files, 321 566 bytes of region, 7 395 tiles, 96 sprite
cells, 768 colours -- each opening with its origin, format, palette and V-RAM
destination, and `assets/manifest.json`, generated by the same command, is the
record that ties every file to the dump by address and hash and names the
screens that use it.

The formats are ticket 181's codec, not a second one: `scripts/extract_assets.py`
calls `scripts/rom_formats.py` to decode and encode, so the files, the pages and
`tests/test_formats.sh` cannot disagree. `--check` decodes every committed file,
re-encodes it and compares it with the cartridge, naming the first asset, byte
and address that does not come back; it also regenerates the manifest, requires
the committed copy to be current, and fails on a file the catalogue does not
know. `tests/test_assets.sh` proves the check can fail with nine mutations and
that a fresh extraction is the committed tree byte for byte, and proves a change
gets back in: `--apply` writes a copy of the image that differs from the dump at
exactly the bytes the edit changed, refuses the dump's own path, and refuses a
compressed edit whose stream would change length.

The review: `--png` writes a sheet per asset (tile sheet, composed map, palette
swatches, sprite frames, the font as the encoder sees it) and `--confirm` prints,
per asset family, those pictures and the checkpoint whose emulator frame they are
judged against. docs/assets.md carries the confirmation record, family by
family, and the three findings the review produced:

  * seven of the fifteen screen-resource rows named the wrong screen -- the
    call sites are constant indices and the frames agree: 14 is the OPTIONS
    screen, 9 the mission-won, 11 the mission-lost, 8 the campaign's ending,
    and 1/2/3/7 are duplicates nothing loads;
  * the title screen's art is resources 12/13's block (the DUNE logo, the
    credits, the planet), whose name table the screen writes;
  * the sprite attribute's `+2` word has its size in bits 8-11, which the
    chunk lengths prove: entry 0's cell is 2x4 tiles and its chunk is $100
    bytes, ending exactly where the next chunk begins.

The files are committed so a change to a picture is a `git diff`; D97 records
that exception to D5b/D6, that the dump, the blobs and the pictures stay out,
and that `rm -rf assets` + `--extract` takes it back out. The byte-exact rebuild
and the existing gates are untouched: the new check is the slow tier's, with the
round trip.
