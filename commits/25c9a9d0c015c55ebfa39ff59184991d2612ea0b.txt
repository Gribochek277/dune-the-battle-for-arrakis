record of what it still runs. `analysis/final-proof.json` is written by

fails when the committed record is not this tree's.

So the epic's "zero guest addresses, zero hardware accesses" is not this tree's
number, and the record says so instead of the prose. The 1 201 600 are the unit
band's forty-eight routines; ticket 227's `unit-band` group (which the status
table records as merged) is not in this tree -- `git show --stat 6b171457` is
`port/Makefile | 1 +`, and neither 6b171457 nor main has `port/next/unit_band.c`
or the fixtures, while main's inventory has 26 groups and 48 guest routines.
That merge is the ticket that would take thirty-six of them; the six gaps above
are the coverage ticket that would take the hardware writes.

The played build's gated steps: the port audio engine is the played path
(ticket 197), and its timing moves the mission's work a frame or a few cycles
apart from the cartridge's driver from frame 7313 (the first per-frame
difference against the same selection on the Z80): the mission, hud,
build-panel and options steps are not the baseline's. The reference arrangement
(next + z80) is. The record carries both, and the page names the engine's
timing as the owner.
