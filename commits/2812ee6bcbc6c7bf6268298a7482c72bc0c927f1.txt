216-named-kinds: kinds are entities with the cartridge's names and one field registry
The game says what a kind is by where a register points; the repository could
read the type tables but had no name for a kind and no place a field's meaning
lived. This ticket makes a kind an entity:

- analysis/descriptor-fields.txt is the one store of a descriptor field's name,
  width, offset and evidence. The extractor, next/kinds.c, the notes and
  docs/data.md read it; scripts/name_check.py fails the gate when
  guest_state.h's accessor, a data line or the notes' table disagree (the
  +4/+6/+8 case is its fixture). unnamed_XX is gone: a field is named
  with a reader, unused_XX (zero everywhere), or unestablished_XX with the
  capture that would settle it.
- Every unit and structure kind has the cartridge's own name: the pointer
  tables at 082DC2 and 0836C0, transcribed on the descriptor addresses in
  analysis/symbol-names.txt, with analysis/kinds-units.png as the picture.
  next/kinds.c is one entity per kind and per terrain class; its guest rows
  are generated by scripts/make_kinds.py from the asm the build assembles, and
  it carries one port-owned kind (NEXT_UNIT_SANDBUGGY) with the guest's limits
  explicit. mission_orders.c reads the kind's flags instead of a descriptor.
- The 512-byte terrain class table moved from seg_code_000218 to
  assets/data/terrain/tile-classes.asm, its 16 codes are game terms in
  analysis/symbol-names.txt (TerrainSpice = #08 ...), and the ported class
  override reads NEXT_TERRAIN_GRAPHICS_OVERRIDE.
- D1 is settled: +0 is the base maximum, CreateMissionForce scales it by the
  placement's fraction, and kind 0D stands at 2x it in every capture; the rules
  page carries the qualification and scripts/game_rules.py measures it.

Gates: scripts/build.sh (42 known header bytes), compare_roms, test_data.sh
(the three-field balance change: six bytes, nothing else), test_segment_split,
test_names, test_docs, test_port_zones, test_pipeline_inputs, the kinds
selftest, game_rules, and the fast run_all suite.
