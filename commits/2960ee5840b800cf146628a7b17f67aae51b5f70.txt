ticket 96: the mapping screen documented for the player, and for the next maintainer
docs/gamepad.md gains the screen in the player's own terms: how to rebind, that a
swap is a swap and not a theft (and is therefore recoverable by doing it again), that
the game does not see the button while a row waits, that a control the pad lacks shows
NOT ON PAD and cannot be bound, and what a failed write looks like -- including the
honest limit, that the map survives a failed save but the instruction to save it does
not, so the player may have to make the change once more.

docs/next-engine.md gains the boundary, which is where all of this feature's design
actually is: why the seam carries two strings and three function pointers and could
not carry anything else without the arrangement becoming the thing it was built
beside; why the field widths are measured from the vocabulary rather than round (both
numbers were found by being wrong first); why capture consumes the control in app.c
before the map is applied rather than undoing it afterwards, when afterwards leaves
one frame in which the game has already read the button; and why the port folds case
at the tile, because the game's font has no lowercase Latin and its first screen to
find that out printed pad_up in Cyrillic.

Both sections keep the failures in the prose. A document that records only the design
that won teaches nobody why the others lost.
