Ticket 148. With a box of units standing, the mouse could not get back to
the one structure under the pointer: ticket 128's click scanned the cell
itself and ticket 129's click was an order, so neither collapsed the set.

The click is now the guest's own selection. The cursor already turns a
click into the pad's A bit at $003118/$003AD0 (ticket 127); what was
missing was the state the guest selects from, so the click's frame writes
0 into $FFFFC25C and clears the +$6 bit 15 marker on the record that stops
being the primary. The guest's $016FF6 then does what it does for a pad A
with nothing selected: a unit into $FFFFC25C with the marker ($0176D0), a
structure into the guest's own $FFFFC578 with the marker ($0170FC),
nothing on empty ground. The port adopts $FFFFC25C as the whole set -- one
unit or nothing -- after the command pass has run, with a bounded wait
(four polls) so a click the guest swallowed still settles. The port no
longer scans a cell for a click at all: click_cell() and cell_at() are
gone, and a click in the widescreen band works because the cursor's record
already carries the band's pixel.

The box's write has to be undone rather than left for the A to overwrite,
and that is measured: with $FFFFC25C still naming the box's primary the
guest's A on a structure takes the $97CB at $01705E, drops the structure
out of a3, and the structure is then not selected at all ($FFFFC578
unchanged, $FFFFC25C = 0).

The group command moved from the click to the pad's A, which is the
cartridge's own order gesture: next_mission_selection_pad_command() is
called by the cursor's C at $003118/$003AD0 with the frame's newly pressed
byte, and with a set standing it marks the primary and re-posts it, so the
guest's own pass gives it the order and next/mission_orders.c copies it to
the rest of the set. The module is ticket 129's, unchanged; only its
trigger is the pad's now, because the click is the selection. The app's
snapshot of the order is a take rather than a before/after compare: the
post happens inside the guest frame, before the poll.

tests/test_group_orders.sh is edited for the new trigger (the box is the
pointer's, the order is the pad's A at the frame the old click's release
landed in); its arrival run replays the route up to the order and no
further, because the recording's later A presses would re-order the group
-- which is the pad's right and not what the arrival measures. The control
build keeps its meaning: with mission_orders.c stubbed out the A orders
only the primary. tests/test_selection_box.sh gains the collapse runs (2
report lines: the box, then 3 -> 1 / 0 / 0) and three pad-vs-pointer
pairs: the box plus a click against the same hover with --press at the
release frame, identical in the 64 KiB outside the guest's stack, the VDP
state, the picture it renders to and the selection ledger; the port's own
report lines are the one difference.
