ticket 104: the measurement I called small and named turned out not to be
Took it, because that is what saying it is small commits you to. One dump of the game's
own sprite table at $F000, decoded in the hardware's format: six entries, monotonic
pattern indices 0x01-0x06, a diagonal chain, plausible attributes. The format read is
sound and emitting it would be easy.

It does not unblock the work, and the reason is the interesting part: the cursor's
pattern is known to the port as a ROM address ($00D5EA) while the table holds a 9-bit
V-RAM pattern index, and converting between them is precisely the work BuildSpriteTable
does -- it depends on where the game loaded that pattern in V-RAM, unmeasured and not
obvious. Entry [1] has Y=184, exactly the cursor record's Y, but X=3 where the record
holds X=120, so it is not the cursor either.

So the decision to stop was right and the reason I gave for it was wrong. A confident
claim about the size of the next step is the same mistake as a confident claim about its
cause, one rung down, and this session has produced six of the first kind.

Writing an entry with a guessed index would have drawn the wrong tile in the right place
-- the same class of defect as ROM written into the name table, which drew 27 pixels of
$0E0E, a colour a Mega Drive cannot produce, and which the owner caught by looking at
the screen.

main green, text marker in place, nothing guessed.
