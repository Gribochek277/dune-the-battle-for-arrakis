Audio: the console's output chain, so the port's WAV is the reference's (ticket 71)
Ticket 70 left one difference the comparator named every window: the port's
high-frequency share was up to 8.7x the reference's on bright FM notes and its
level up to +8.4 dB on a streamed effect. The chips were not the cause (ticket
68 proved them sample-exact against BlastEm's own); what happens after them was.
The reference recording is BlastEm's mixed output, and BlastEm runs every audio
source through a one-pole lowpass (render_audio.c's lowpass_sample(),
audio.lowpass_cutoff = 3390 Hz in the pinned default.cfg) before it mixes it and
converts the mix to 16 bits.

port/runtime/lowpass.c is that pole: BlastEm's coefficient arithmetic
(0x10000 * dt/(dt+rc), rc from the cutoff, dt from the source's own period), its
int16 state and its truncation. sound.c owns one instance per source, stepped
where the chip's level changes -- the PSG at its 223721.5625 Hz tick (alpha
5697), the YM2612 at its 53267.0387 Hz sample rate (alpha 18720) -- so the box
filter's sample count and the WAV's shape are untouched. The truncation is kept
because it is visible: the YM2612's raw zero-offset floor of 438 settles at 435,
which is the reference recording's own floor (measured on its silent first
seconds).

The other half was the PSG's own level. BlastEm's PSG source carries the chip's
unipolar output at the documented attenuator table / 14 (its PSG_VOL_DIV), where
the port had a centred square at table >> 3: +4.9 dB of amplitude and another
3 dB of RMS from the waveform. The port's PSG now follows BlastEm's -- unipolar,
/14 -- which also puts the reference's DC in the port's samples. That is a
deliberate departure from the physically accurate model, which psg.h records:
the console AC-couples the chip's output, but the port's reference is the
recording, and the recording carries the chip's own unipolar level.

Measured on the 132 s mission pair (the 7898-frame run of ticket 70): the two
files' total RMS +2.11 -> +0.01 dB; the above-3390 Hz share 4.7..8.7x ->
1.0..1.7x; level-failing windows 47 -> 12 of 528; windows within 3 dB and 50
cents 180 -> 185 strict and 257 -> 270 with --local-shift 0.02 (280 at the
measured +36 ms offset, against 262 before). Every remaining level failure is a
DAC burst's or a PSG effect's boundary -- each is within 0.9 dB of the reference
over a 1-second window at +36 ms -- plus the two windows after the recording's
frame-4298 press, which tickets 65/66 own. The 300-frame house-selection gate
still passes, with its envelope correlation at +0.869 against +0.855.

Fixtures: port/tests/test_lowpass.c pins the coefficients against BlastEm's own
expression, the exact step response at both rates, the DC fixed point and its
truncation bound, and the -3 dB point measured from swept tones (2861 Hz on the
YM2612, 3242 Hz on the PSG; the coefficient's own are 2879 and 3240 -- the
pinned 3390 Hz parameter through BlastEm's backward-Euler discretization, which
is what the port follows, not the hardware's). test_psg.c and test_ym2612.c
measure their chips' levels through that chain, with the reason recorded where
an expectation moved. The runtime still needs no libm; only the test binaries
link it, for the swept-tone fixture.

docs/native-port.md has the section with the table of remaining windows and the
commands that produce them; bash tests/run_all.sh ends with ALL CHECKS PASSED
and the cartridge stays byte-exact.
