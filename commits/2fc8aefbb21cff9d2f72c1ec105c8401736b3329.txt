D152: the GPU overlay is written whole when its texture is new, and not at all when nothing changed
The owner's playtest on a 2560x1440 display: after leaving the build panel the
mission was a black screen, repainted only where the pointer moved, and the
whole game felt slow on the large screen.

* The picture's layer width changes between the mission's wide world and the
  panel's 4:3 picture, create_targets() recreates every target including the
  overlay's texture, and the next present uploaded only the pointer's dirty
  rectangles into it: the rest was the driver's leftover memory, opaque black
  on Intel. A new texture is now uploaded whole.
* An empty upload list meant 'upload the whole layer': every picture with no
  shape on it re-specified the target-sized texture, 14.7 MB at 2560x1440,
  sixty times a second. An empty list is now nothing to upload, and a whole
  upload uses glTexSubImage2D on the existing storage.

At 2560x1440 fullscreen the GPU's work per picture is 0.94 ms (was 3.65) and
84 of 900 pictures drop with vsync (was 283). present-gl-test gains the
layer-width case. tests/test_present_gpu.sh's trail comparison is timing-flaky
on this machine with or without the change (old binary: 1 of 3 runs same).
