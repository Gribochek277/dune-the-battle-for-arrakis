ticket 101: one criterion restated because it is impossible, one left open because it is a question
**Restated and ticked.** The value-line criterion asked that the cells a value line
would occupy be left as the backdrop left them. That cannot be satisfied: this screen
writes plane B, where an untouched cell is tile zero, and tile zero of this backdrop is
a solid colour -- leaving them as they were is exactly what produced the olive stripes.
Transparency on plane B needs a cell deliberately written. The provable form of the
rule, plane A byte-for-byte against the game's own options screen, was already
asserted, so the criterion was wrong and the test was right.

**Measured and left open.** The attribute criterion asked for the game's own attribute
at the game's own positions. Measured: the glyphs and palette match (palette 3, colour
8, the same white), and the selected row comes from state.cursor -- the same field that
draws >, so the two cannot disagree. But the port writes plane B at $C000 where the
game's menu is plane A, so its cells carry $E000 where the game's carry $6000, and
its labels start at column 8 rather than the game's column. Both follow from the
settled design -- the port borrows assets, not positions -- which makes this criterion
a question about that design rather than a task. Rewording it away would be the quiet
kind of wrong, so it stays open with the numbers in it.

The priority bit doing nothing on plane B is why the $E000 difference is not a colour
bug: with plane A holding no priority, nothing can be drawn under the port's text.
