ticket 102: split the gate into tiers -- measured, not felt
The owner asked for this as a task rather than a change right now, so it is a task
with the numbers in it: 45 checks, 1668 s, and three checks at 811 s between them --
49% of the whole gate in 7% of its files. tests/test_port_settings_screen.sh drives
headless-fast eleven times, up to frame 3000.

The criteria carry the two constraints that make this a reorganisation rather than a
speedup: the byte-exactness and engine-equivalence gates stay in the fast tier no
matter what they cost, and nothing is deleted, weakened or loosened to make a tier
go green. The timing method is written down too (wrap the suite's own run() with
date +%s) so the next session starts from a measurement instead of an impression.

Note for whoever picks it up: tests/run_all.sh already honours an ONLY filter, so
partial runs exist; what is missing is a fast default and a named slow tier.
