177: the playtest route, its baseline and every selection
The route is one committed recording (analysis/recordings/playtest.pad): boot,
the intro, the title screen, the house selection, the briefing, the mission with
the base's panel and the mission's OPTIONS -- the Atreides route's presses
unchanged through entry 9 498 so its catalog checkpoints are reached in the same
state, plus a three-press tail that leaves the panel and opens OPTIONS, which is
the one replacement group the Atreides route alone never reaches.

analysis/playtest-route.json is the record: ten steps, the reference
arrangement's whole step line at each (the baseline), the legal mixes with the
difference each may or must answer and why, and the mixes that cannot work with
the name the refusal has to carry. scripts/playtest_route.py reads, checks and
regenerates it; scripts/compare_state_logs.py names the first frame two runs
part at; scripts/playtest.sh is the one command a person plays.

The harness gained --step FRAME[:PATH] / --step-log PATH: the step line is the
per-frame digest plus the work RAM outside the stack (the top 1 KiB is the
stack, the project's convention), and a step with a path is also a machine state
the window resumes with --debug-load. The front ends now name the selection they
refuse (a group on legacy, an unknown group, --no-replace next to a group).

tests/test_playtest_route.sh is the gate: the record against the tree, the
reference and the guest's own code frame by frame over the whole route, the
baseline byte for byte, every mix inside the picture and ram-outside-stack
gates, the groups the route reaches measured from --flag-inventory, the
refusals by name, the playtest command's snapshots and resume commands, the
port's own screen and the widescreen mission in the window, and seven negative
controls. About three minutes in the fast tier.

docs/collecting-coverage.md has the human half (what to press, what to look at,
what is the port's C on the way); D94 records the route, the baseline and the
gates.
