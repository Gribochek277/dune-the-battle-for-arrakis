knows which of 'the player cancelled' and 'the pad was pulled' just happened, and
criterion 6 asks for the second to be *reported*. Without it the screen goes back to
inviting a press on hardware that has left the machine: the player obliges, nothing
happens, and no line on screen says why.

against the device that is actually there, and the status line says GAMEPAD
DISCONNECTED.

once, and a screen with no operations cannot be captured at all. These are the cases
a virtual joystick cannot enumerate: the ones where nothing is pressed. The
consume-once check is the difference between a swap and a runaway -- a second control
after the edit must not be a second edit.
