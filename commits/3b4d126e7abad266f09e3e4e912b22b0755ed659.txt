The console's DRAM refresh is part of the clock: 2 cycles every 128
BlastEm charges the 68000 2 cycles per 128 for the VDP's DRAM refresh
(genesis.c, REFRESH_INTERVAL/REFRESH_DELAY, gen_update_refresh()), which is
1.5625 % of the guest's time.  The port's cycle model documented it as a
deliberate omission, so the port's guest got that much more work done per
VBlank than the emulator's, and the frame boundary landed at a different
point inside the guest's frame.

md68k_cycles_charge() is now the instruction path: the instruction's cycles
plus the refresh charge, with the remainder carried the way BlastEm's
refresh_counter carries it.  md68k_cycles_add() stays the raw advance for a
caller that owns the clock.  md68k_cycles_set_refresh(0), and the harness's
--no-refresh, reproduce the old clock exactly: the control the mission-turn
check runs.

Measured at VBlank entry 7700 of the Atreides recording, the mission's own
counters ($FFFFC594/$FFFFC598) move from 8 entries ahead of the reference to
4, the order timer at record 33's +$14 from $10 to the reference's $11, and
the RandomSeed from 99B7E5 (420 RandomByte calls) to 53E63B, which is the
reference's state at the same 426 calls.  The checkpoint catalog: mission-map
182 -> 23 pixels (the credit counter's last digit), in-game-hud 152 -> 0,
build-panel 100 -> 0, briefing-text 0 with its gate moved to pixel-exact.

The fixture: 32 NOPs cost 130 cycles, 320 cost 1300, and without the charge
128; the boot stub's total moves with it.
