ticket 224: the switch table was not a routine -- the split withdraws it, the row takes the case bodies, and a branch entry is answered with the guest's instruction
Measured on analysis/recordings/fremen-won.pad at entry 16 541: the guest is at
$0169A8 with d0=$0E (the offset it read from the table at $01699A), d4 the map
cell, d5 the tile class and a7 in sub_0168FE's own frame, and the instruction
there is movea.l $FFFFC064,a1 (six bytes, 16 cycles). $01699A is the switch's
seven-word offset table -- read by move.w/jmp ($01699A,pc,d0.w) at $016992/
$016996 and guarded to 0..6 -- and the sweep had decoded it as instructions,
with ori.w #$2278,(a4)+ at $0169A6 swallowing the first case body's first word.

So the fix is the split's: $01699A-$0169A7 goes to analysis/data-tables.txt with
the evidence, seg_code_0169A8 begins at the real first instruction, sub_0168FE's
row takes the whole Ghidra body ($0168FE-$016AB5) and sub_01699A leaves the
replacement table (604 rows, the band 53). The generated translation, the
briefing part and the guest boundary are regenerated; the image is the dump.

The mechanism keeps its four entry kinds and refuses zero cost as before, but the
line now names the address the guest reached, how far into the span, and the kind
('entered by a call'), and port/tests/test_replacement.c proves both the answered
span entry and the named refusal, for all four kinds. There is deliberately no
sentinel for a legitimate zero: the guest's entry costs 16 cycles.

The win route enters three of the briefing's rows the other routes did not
(sub_0168FE, sub_016AB6, sub_016AE8): their fixtures are captured from it in
oracle_fixtures.sh's ended-mission stage, the manifest carries their hashes, and
they move from UNMEASURED to MEASURED (43 of 53). The four rows the route does
not enter keep their declaration, which says what it showed. make_symbols.py
drops a code name whose address the split gave to data and refuses a container
name for a code segment that begins inside a routine, so the routine index cannot
inherit the phantom. tests/test_flag_groups.sh walks the full catalogue again:
the 218 exclusion, its rot-check and the 'left out by name' note are gone, and
mission-won is back in the two-selection comparison.
