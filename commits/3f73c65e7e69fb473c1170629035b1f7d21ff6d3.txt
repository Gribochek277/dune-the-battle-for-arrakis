The clock charges the Z80's bus and the VBlank entry: 8 a window access, 34 a frame
BlastEm charges the 68000 for four things this port did not: a Z80 access
through its banked window (z80_read_bank()/z80_write_bank(): 8 * MCLKS_PER_68K,
and the VDP window too), the one-cycle wait state an access to the Z80 area
costs (io_read()/io_write(): MCLKS_PER_68K), the VBlank entry (the core's 24
cycles of exception frame plus int_ack()'s 5 + cycles mod 10, charged as its
mean 10) and the wait a data-port write takes when the VDP's four-word FIFO is
full (FIFO_SIZE/FIFO_LATENCY: one word per 32 master clocks).

Each is a #define in BlastEm's sources with the file named in timing.h, each has
its own fixture in port/tests/test_cycles.c with a control that fails without
it, and md68k_cycles_statistics() now reports the run's clock as the buckets --
instruction, refresh, the Z80's window, the Z80 area, the VDP FIFO, the VBlank
entry and any raw time a caller added -- so the itemisation is a measurement.
The harness prints them and writes them as port.clock in the state dump;
--no-contention is the kill switch for all four (the control the checks use).

Measured on the Atreides recording to entry 7898: the mission clock $FFFFC594
moves 212 -> 210 against the reference's 208, the credit roll
$FFFFD6C0/$FFFFD6BC 986 + 2/8 -> 986 + 0/8, and since that fraction is zero the
drawn digit is the reference's 986: analysis/checkpoints.json's mission-map
entry reads 0 of 71 680 pixels, the catalog's ninth. The Z80's window accesses
are 481 934 over the run against the reference's 484 584, measured by rebuilding
BlastEm 1.0.0 with the same counters (0.5 % apart, so the constant and not a
number is what was missing); the remaining two entries of clock lead are the
reference's own VDP FIFO service under its slot scheduling, measured and named
in docs/native-port.md.

The port's DMA model is left as it was: BlastEm stops the 68000 for a 68k -> VDP
transfer and charges the whole transfer at the command word, this sets the busy
bit and lets the guest's poll pay for the same time (11 063 483 cycles against
the reference's 11 556 114). Charging it as a stall changes the guest's
instruction stream without changing the clock, so it is recorded rather than
done.

The boot stub's pinned total moves by its 38 Z80-area accesses (0x5A344 ->
0x5A36A), which is what the new wait state is worth there.
