ticket 135: display settings on the port's screen -- scale, aspect, fullscreen
The port's settings screen gains a display section: the interface's scale, the
world's scale, the aspect mode (auto, 4:3, wide, fit-height) and fullscreen or
windowed, as four more rows of the menu template (their values, their states'
locale words and the front end's read/write seam, with no navigation code), plus
the file they persist in and the flags that override it.

The values are the run's (app/settings.h), the four rows are the screen's, and
what a target shows is now a plan: app/display_layout.c resolves the values and
a target into a mode and two integer scales and builds the layout from it, so a
change made on the running screen reflows the next frame with no restart and a
plan the model cannot build is the nearest one that can. app/display_file.c is
display.ini beside the gamepad map -- closed vocabularies, a bad line refused
with its number, a save that writes all four -- and --interface-scale,
--world-scale, --aspect-mode, --fullscreen/--no-fullscreen and --display-file
override it for one run.

The interface stays pixel-correct at an integer scale and the world has its own
scale (auto = the interface's, which is the wide mission's rule), so a forced
world scale changes how much world shows rather than the picture's height. That
made the mission's layout hold its interface regions against the target's edges
at the interface's scale instead of measuring them from the world picture's
rectangle: byte-identical at equal scales, and the HUD no longer follows a
smaller world into the middle of the screen. 4:3 and fit-height differ only on a
target narrower than 4:3 (fitted inside it, or scaled to its height and
cropped); full-screen screens are the 4:3 picture with bars in every mode.

Tests: port/tests/test_display_file.c and test_display_layout.c (the file's
format and every mode's arithmetic, in the selftest), test_settings_screen.c and
test_row_lists.c (seven rows, the display values' words and the seam),
tests/test_display_settings.sh (the front end: the file read and overridden, an
invalid value refused with its line, the portrait target telling 4:3 from
fit-height, a live change reflowing inside the run, the 16:9 mission staying 4:3
when the file says so, the pad, a scripted mouse and real keys on a private X
display each operating a row, fullscreen from the file and from the flags, and
the reviewed screenshot analysis/reference/port-display-settings.png). The
mission-wide and shell checks now name a display file of their own so a player's
cannot move what they measure; tests/test_port_settings_screen.sh follows the
way out to the seventh row of the five-line window and the display rows' cells.

Two test-suite defects the change exposed are fixed here: the direct-compile
test binaries now depend on the headers (a stale test object built against an
older words table segfaulted the locale test), and words.c's three tables are
unsized so the typedef that compares them with the key count catches a table one
line short -- args was, which is a template that is never checked.

Docs: docs/display.md (the player's page), D62, and the widescreen,
next-engine, port-architecture and build-and-test pages; the site is rebuilt.
port/README.md is at its 64 KiB bound (65 536 B) and docs/port-architecture.md
records that the next module added there has to be a split, not a row.
