ticket 101: the plane-A assertion the value-line criterion stands on compares the wrong range
Found while re-restating the position criterion, measured off the same dumps, and
written down rather than fixed here -- the fix is a decision about what the reference
is, not a range edit.

tests/test_port_settings_screen.sh's "plane A name table" check compares V-RAM
$2000..$2E00. Plane A's base is $6000 (register 2 = $18, (reg2 & 0x38) << 10), and
$2000 is the dead SETTINGS_ART_BASE 0x00002000u constant in settings_screen.c. The
compared range is inside the tile region the "backdrop tiles" check already compares,
so that assertion passes for a reason unrelated to plane A.

Measured: the port's screen holds $6000..$6FFF equal to the resource's own tilemap,
byte for byte (the tilemap is at ROM $053234) -- and the game's own options screen
adds $2420 across rows 28..31, so comparing the port against the game's screen would
not hold either without saying which of the two is the reference.

The criterion itself is untouched: this records that its evidence is weaker than its
note claims, so the next reader does not have to find that out from scratch.
