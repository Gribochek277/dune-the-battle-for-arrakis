docs: the prose lives in the wiki (D158, ticket 229)
The markdown left the repository: `docs/wiki` is a submodule of the project's
Gitea wiki, and the pages are published there with `scripts/wiki.py publish`.
The site keeps being generated -- now from the submodule -- and its gate keeps
proving the pages, their links and their anchors, so the reading and the checks
did not change; what changed is where a person edits.

Mechanical where it could be:

* the prose's own links are relative page names (`build-and-test.md`) and needed
  no rewriting: `docs/wiki/<name>.md` sits at the same depth as
  `docs/<name>.md` did, and Gitea and the site resolve the same targets;
* the citations (`docs/x.md` in 262 files) became `docs/wiki/x.md`, which is
  what a reader who follows one finds in the checkout -- 677 replacements in
  hand-written and generated files alike, so a regeneration agrees;
* six consumers of the old paths learned the new one: `build_docs` (PROSE,
  RULES_DOC, the extras glob), `name_check`, `porting_stages`, the audit table's
  `AUDIT` in tests/test_pipeline_inputs.py, and the page paths in
  tests/test_modding.sh and tests/test_names.py;
* the site maps the wiki's mirrored diagram (`docs/wiki/html/...`, where a wiki
  page's image link has to resolve) to its own build, and
  `tests/test_terminology.sh` reads the submodule's files: the parent's
  `git ls-files` lists the gitlink and not the pages, so D144 would have stopped
  checking exactly where the documents are written now;
* the wiki's shell and the generator's pages are not documents: `doc_plans`
  skips `Home`, `_Sidebar`, the three root mirrors and the `GENERATED` prefixes,
  so the site's 35 document pages stay 35.
