ticket 219: the build's own state, constructed — fifteen rows the pad cannot reach
Tickets 207 and 210 left 52 rows declared rather than faked; ticket 218 takes
the 32 a mission played to its end explains. These 20 are the rest, and fifteen
of them are the structure band's command, production, placement and per-kind
handlers: no ending, win or loss, makes a factory's timer complete, cancels a
build or places the queue the yard creates.

What the pad can reach was measured, not inferred. The mission recording opens
the build panel (RunStructurePanel runs, $FFFFBF8E fills with EXIT/FIX/STOP and
two items, ApplyStructureCommand answers), and adding 'down' then 'A' to its own
recording makes the yard queue a build (slot 125, credits 990 -> 975, the yard's
+$6 word 0x8010 -> 0xA010, +$3 = 125). The queue is placed by a map click whose
footprint fits; a 48-cell scripted walk of the explored island entered the
placement and failed the footprint test everywhere, and the failure path freed
the queue. That is the state the mechanism refuses: a route would have to play a
whole mission's economy, and it would still not reach the handlers for kinds the
mission's script never places.

So the state is constructed, in port/tests/test_structures.c: the guest's own
code runs from the record the game would have, then the whole `structures` group
runs as the port's C from the same bytes, and every register, the 64 KiB of work
RAM, the VDP's write counters and the clock are compared. Each case names the
rows it must have entered and fails when the C never answered one: the build's
cancel and the panel's helpers behind it, a build's last payment (the progress
byte reaching zero), a completed placement's scan, the placement guard's other
arm, a factory's production half and its queue handler, and one record per kind
for the six per-record handlers (the one that waits in a helper is compared
instruction for instruction instead).

The split stays the review: tests/test_oracle.sh gains CONSTRUCTED beside
MEASURED and UNMEASURED and refuses a constructed row that has a fixture or
whose named case is not in the test; tests/test_structures_band.sh and the
structures manifest carry the same fifteen, and 39 + 15 + 4 is the band's 58.
The four left are the campaign end (ticket 218). sub_00C69E keeps its
declaration with the state named: its arm creates an AI group and no mission's
script makes a group record, so no pad on a recorded route presents it.

docs/decisions.md D126 records the decision and the measurement behind it.
