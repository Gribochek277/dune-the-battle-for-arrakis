156: a pointer click selects the record under it, not its map cell
The cartridge's click asks which record is in the cursor's cell (GetUnitByCell
through the cell snap sub_01E208), which is the d-pad's target and a 32-pixel
one: the unit icon is 16x16, so a click a few pixels beside it selected the unit
anyway. The pointer now has its own hit test and the pad's cell stays the pad's.

next_mission_selection_pointer_at() asks which record is *drawn* under the
point: the units GetUnitByCell names in the pointer's cell and its eight
neighbours, a unit a target while the point is inside a 24-pixel square around
its drawn position (the icon's 8 plus four pixels of grace -- smaller than the
sprite is a click a player aims and misses, and 12 is still inside the cell's
16), a structure a target on the tiles it holds. The nearest wins (squared
distance to the record's drawn centre), a unit before a structure at the same
distance, then the lower record index.

The click is still the guest's selection: the profile hands it to the guest's
own A path, and when the target is in another cell than the hand's it aims the
click first by putting the cursor on the record's cell's corner -- the pixel the
guest's cell lookup names that cell from. A click beside a record the guest
would have selected is not delivered at all; $FFFFC25C becomes 0 with the
primary's marker off and the port's set empties in one step. Open ground (no
unit in the nine cells) is still handed to the guest's own empty-ground path.

the unit from the point the pointer calls a miss);
