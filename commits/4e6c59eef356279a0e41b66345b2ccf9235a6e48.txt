tests: the playtest route allows the wide view's vertical and cursor bounds (D156)
The route's wide check compares the 4:3 and 16:9 runs' work RAM at entry 62
of the mission step. Any difference outside the wide view's own writes fails
it. Under D156 the wide view writes more of the bounds than before:

* the camera's vertical words $FFFFE3CE/$FFFFE3D0 move by the signed lines
  the viewport shows. At 854x480 the border's 9 and 7 lines are the world's
  now, so the words differ (measured: $FFE3CF and $FFE3D1).
* the cursor record's clamps $FFFFBF1A..$FFFFBF21 move with the extra.

The allowed set now names the four camera words, the clamps and the sprite
table. The cartridge builds the table from a cursor record that the wider
clamps can place differently; before D156 the table also held the port's
appended band entries. The assertion is edited with that reason, not
dropped. Nothing else in work RAM may differ.
