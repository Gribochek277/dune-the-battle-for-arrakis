WIP ticket 100: the screen renders its own title and the backdrop's edges
On wip/ticket-100-port-screen only. The root cause of the flat screen is found and
fixed -- a VDP command must be one WORD write, because the model decodes a register
write from the first word and returns, while a long leaves it pending and swallows
the next command's first word. With that fixed all seventeen VDP registers match the
options screen's exactly, and the picture is the title plus the artwork at the left
and right edges.

What is left is the loader's coverage, not the mechanism: the middle and bottom of
the backdrop are missing, and measuring where they belong is unreliable by
inspection because the resource is full of repeated blank tiles -- ROM $053234's
first 32 bytes match V-RAM at $5A40, $5A42 and $5A46, which is zeros, not a
destination. The next attempt should read the destination out of LoadScreenResource's
selector table rather than infer it from a byte pattern.
