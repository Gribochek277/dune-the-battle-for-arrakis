228: every row records its guest's instructions, and a dispatch advances the sound and the Z80 once
The comparison arrangement cost 3.01 ms a frame against the guest's 2.75 --
9 % slower than interpreting the cartridge, on the path nobody plays but every
group check runs. Measured before anything was changed, none of the gap was the
Z80 driver's bus contention: the harness's own clock reports 368 Z80-window
accesses (2 944 cycles against the frame's 128 006) on both paths, to the
access. The mechanism's own lookup is +0.21 ms a frame with nothing replaced
(legacy against --no-replace), paid by both paths.

What it was: the seam advanced the sound renderer and the Z80 once per recorded
guest instruction and then advanced both again at the end of the step, and 2.4 %
of the dispatches (73 000 in 250 frames) reported more cost than they recorded,
so the fallback charged the remainder one 68000 cycle at a time -- 800 496 extra
advances, 40 instruction slots of every guest instruction of the frame.

What changed:

* the twenty-five files that charged the bare cost model move to
  next_replacement_charge() (1 393 sites), so their rows record their steps and
  the per-cycle fallback never runs on a committed route. Charging the remainder
  as one step instead was measured and rejected: it moves the refresh phase
  against the Z80's bus charges (two cycles over 9 500 frames of the leaf
  helpers' route, and the timeline after them).
* md68k_step() asks the body whether the arrangement already advanced the clock,
  so an instruction's sound step and Z80 run are paid once.
* next_replacement_call is the CPU and the address: its frame point, clock and
  pad fields were filled with four calls per guest instruction and no row read
  one. The refusals build their sentences out of the hot frame, the lookup
  answers the row's table index with the row and remembers the last answer, and
  the dispatch's hot path is one inline.

The record is guest 2.61 ms, comparison 2.52 and played 2.23 on this machine:
the comparison build draws 1.04x the guest's frames per CPU second and the
played one 1.17x. The check measures the guest and the comparison in paired
rounds with the order alternated, keeping the fastest run of eight a path,
because they are now within a few percent and one sample measures the host; the
comparison margin is 1.0 again. Work RAM, picture, counters, sound and the Z80
timeline are identical to the build before this ticket on the comparison and
played runs, and the engine equivalence pair still holds.

D134 in docs/decisions.md is the record; docs/port-architecture.md and D130's
number now read from the record through {{fact:...}} instead of retyping it.
