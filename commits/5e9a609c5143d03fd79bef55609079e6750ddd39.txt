WIP ticket 100: the screen loads its picture through the game's own routines
Four real bugs found and fixed, and the screen now loads the guest's resource,
palette and font and draws its title over the artwork:

* md68k_jsr pushes next_address(pc), which is right from $00D5A4 and wrong from
  $00D5A6 -- it pushes $00D5A8, the middle of a four-byte instruction. The screen
  now names its own return address and every call lands on the dispatcher.
* The guest's argument convention is 'write at the caller's (a7), then jsr', which
  means the caller owns that word. Here the title screen's saved d3 owns it, so the
  screen takes a scratch slot of its own and gives it back on the way out.
* A VDP register write must be one word; a long leaves the model pending and eats the
  next command's first word.
* Setting the plane bases is not enough: the backdrop's tilemap carries the priority
  bit in every cell, so plane A hides plane B wherever it has a pixel. That is why
  the title shows and the rows did not -- the rows were in memory, correct, and
  underneath the artwork.

What is left is one measurement: where on the text plane this screen's rows go so
that they are not under the artwork. The first two guesses (the menu's rows at row
20, then rows 4-8) are both wrong, and the title rendering at a row that does not
match the arithmetic says the plane's row stride or the vertical scroll is not what
port_screen.h assumes for this base. That is the next thing to measure, and it is
measurement and not design.
