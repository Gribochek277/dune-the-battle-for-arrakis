179: the units, their orders and the AI are one script machine, measured on a battle
Understanding pass 5. The order and AI region is no longer a count of unnamed
instructions: a unit's "order" is a 56-byte entry into which the cartridge loads
a program of 16-bit words, and one interpreter (RunOrderScript $00CBFC) runs it
in the same three blocks the image keeps -- 27 unit-type programs, 19 structure
programs and 6 AI strategies. The words name 19 built-in operations and 19 game
actions (a 64-entry table at $022750), and the script's own helper library (20
entries at $000840) is how a program reads its unit: facing +$6A, target +$5A,
hit-point ratio, side, type, build progress +$71.

The four hypotheses the earlier passes carried are settled with the capture that
exercises each, and two are corrected:
  - AimUnitTurret's +$68/+$6B are the *steps* of two angle triples; the angles
    are +$69/+$6A (body) and +$6C/+$6D (turret), and TurnAngleTriple turns them.
  - SetUnitTarget's +$5A is the target handle and +$5C a *different* handle --
    the structure/or-destination link SetUnitStructureLink writes.
  - SetUnitBuildProgress's +$71 is the build progress and +$70/+$6E the 4.4
    speed step the movement tick reads.
  - CompleteUnitConversion's +$8 <- +$74 switch is the *undo* of ConvertUnit's
    deviator capture (+$5F = $78): the capture is temporary.

The per-frame pass is six time slices, measured: a 3-frame body-turn-and-move,
a 2-frame turret turn, a 20-frame aim/refresh, a 5-frame order program, a
5-frame map-cell/build-progress repaint and a 60-frame conversion check. The
unit record's remaining offsets are named or declared out of scope with the
observation that would settle each.

The AI layer is the same machine with a 15-action vocabulary (7 group records at
$FF789C, created by a mission-script op): AiScanForTarget, AiSelectTarget,
AiCommandGroup, AiRegroupUnits, AiRallyUnits and the four group-record routines
are named and documented, and the finding that matters is that **no committed
mission creates a group** -- the records are zero for the whole Atreides mission
and the mission-script op is never reached.

Evidence: a new route, analysis/recordings/atriedes-battle.pad, enters the
mission and sends no input for 26 000 frames. The mission recording's unit pass
stops at frame 8 298 (its build panel); the battle route runs 17 745 unit-pass
dispatches, 12 095 order-program words, 8 583 movement ticks and 334 damage
dispatches, and its 11 fixtures move DamageUnit, CellDistance and
FindFreeAdjacentCell from the oracle's unmeasured list. The interpreter's and
its movement tick's fixtures are withdrawn with the reason the corpus found: the
sound path reads the Z80 driver's queue index at $A00036 and the corpus seeds
work RAM only, so both rows are declared unmeasured with the bound that would
settle them.

The split now claims the interpreter's handler tails and the AI's cold halves as
code (measured 0.83-1.00 by scan_code_likeness.py), which is why the region's
denominator grows with its share: $01A000 goes from 0 of 2 424 (0 %) to 702 of
2 633 (26.7 %) instructions inside a curated name, $01C000 from 13.6 % to
38.9 %, and the image from 42.4 % to 45.8 %. 35 curated names, 823 routines
(287 named), 338 names, 58 fixtures; the site and both tiers are green.
