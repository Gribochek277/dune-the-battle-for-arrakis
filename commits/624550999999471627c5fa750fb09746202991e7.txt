ticket 157: the picture and its interface are one composition, placed centred
The owner's playtest report: fullscreen 1920x1200, the interface at 2x and the
world at 1x, and the mission's map drawn small -- correct, that is what the
world scale asks -- but against the target's right edge with a 1473-pixel black
bar to its left. The cause was a rule from tickets 132 and 135 that was true
only while the world fills the target's width: the world's right edge was held
to the target's (it *is* the guest's screen's right edge, which is what the
layer map's anchors were measured against) and the interface's margins were
measured from the target's edges. A WORLD scale smaller than the interface's
makes the picture narrower than the target, and every rule that anchored to the
target then anchored to the black bar.

The layout gained a composition: the rectangle its regions are laid out in, in
target pixels -- the guest's 320x240 at the interface's scale -- so an interface
margin is a distance to the picture's own edge at every integer scale and every
world scale. The wide mission states its own in app/wide_mission.c, fit-height's
is its picture, and a layout that states none is placed against the target
itself, which is what every layout that predates this ticket does.

The group -- the rectangle the regions occupy together, their union, so the
world's band is in it and a world drawn smaller than the composition does not
shrink it -- is then placed in the target as one thing: centred on both axes
when it fits, the leftover split with floor so an odd pixel is a bar and never a
stretched one; and when it does not fit, the wide world keeps its right edge on
the target's (D61's rule, the only one that keeps the measured anchors on
screen) while fit-height keeps showing the picture's middle. A group that fills
the target has an offset of 0, so 16:9 at 2x -- the target the layer map was
measured on -- does not move by a pixel, and the checkpoints (4:3, fit-height,
legacy) cannot move.

The reported case: the world 447x240 at 736,478, the group 447x384 at 736,408,
equal bars on both axes where there was one 1473-pixel bar. 21:9's 226-pixel
left bar became 113 on each side, 1080p's 132/120 became 66/60, and the
assertions that read those destinations are edited with this reason (D31/D36),
not deleted. The fast fixtures report the resolved group for the reported case
and its two neighbours (the world at the interface's scale, and a target the
composition fills), and tests/test_mission_wide.sh runs the reported case in the
window and reads the same line out of the log.

The log's layout line now says both scales and how the group was placed
(`group WxH at X,Y (centred)`); the old wide line printed the world's scale
under the words "interface scale", which was invisible while the two always
matched. docs/widescreen.md, docs/display.md and docs/native-port.md describe
the composition and the group, CONTEXT.md has the two words, D72 records the
decision (D71 is ticket 153's, merged first) and the clauses of D61/D62 it
supersedes, and the docs site is regenerated.
