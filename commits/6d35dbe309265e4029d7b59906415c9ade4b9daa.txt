The glossary and the port's README name the scene, the scene backend and the alpha
CONTEXT.md is the project's vocabulary, and ticket 118 added three words the next
tickets (119's blend, 120's layout, 121's GPU backend) will use: the **scene**
(the VDP's picture as data -- a view, not a state store, and not a surface), the
**scene backend** (a module that draws one, resolving no registers), and **alpha**
(how a backend mixes the scene's two states). It also names the **renderer seam**
and the **software renderer** as the oracle, because the scene sits *beside* them
and the distinction is the decision D39 records.

port/README.md's zone and module tables gain the three modules and `--renderer
scene`, the tests table gains `scene-test`, the fixture list gains the scene's,
and vdp-render's row and example gain `--renderer NAME` and `--alpha N`.
