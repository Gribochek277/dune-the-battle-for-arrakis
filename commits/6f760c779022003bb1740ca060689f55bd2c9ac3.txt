analysis: name the 29 most-called routines and 22 RAM and table locations
The symbol table was 2 013 mechanically generated names plus three curated
ones; it is now 54 curated names, and the regenerated sources spell them out
(`jsr (WaitVBlank).l`, `move.w (VBlankCounter).w`, `lea (MapCells).l,a0`).

Every name is backed by an observation recorded in
docs/reverse-engineering-notes.md ("M2-1"), never by a guess about what the
game must do. The findings that carry the pass:

* the V-blank frame clock: the handler at $0016CC increments $FFFFE006 and
  $FFFFE01A at $001704/$001708, calls the frame handler whose pointer lives at
  $FFFFE002, drains the DMA queue at $FFFFE02A through $000D60, and plays the
  queued sound ids through the driver pointer at ROM $024024; the seven
  reference captures show $FFFFE006 = frame-1 at frame N and the queue
  pointers moving in steps of $0A, equal exactly when the queue is idle
* the DMA queue: $000A06 appends 10-byte entries and waits for a V-blank when
  the queue is full, $0012C2 waits for it to drain, $000D60 is the V-blank
  side; the write/read pointers, the 64-entry wrap at $280 and the flag byte
  are all named
* two tagged record classes: a unit handle is $4000 | index and a structure
  handle is $8000 | index, which is what the accept rules of $000680/$000610
  encode (the argument is doubled twice and used as a word index, so the tag
  falls out modulo 65536), what $00069A builds explicitly, and what $000762
  validates; the map keeps 4-byte cells whose object word has bit 13 and bit 12
  for the two classes and whose low byte is the index
* the fixed arrays: 128 unit records of $8C bytes at $FF0000, 128 structure
  records of $62 bytes at $FF4574 and five side records of $82 bytes at
  $FF7612, each addressed through a pointer table whose entries are exactly
  base + k * size
* the rest: the 3-byte LFSR at $FFFFE017, the sound wrappers and their
  deferred command queue, the VDP register-1 shadow, the text writer's VRAM
  base, the two arithmetic helpers and the map bounds check

Two limits are recorded rather than papered over: the 98-byte records are
called structures on the strength of the two-class code structure (their game
meaning needs a post-mission capture to confirm), and the naming pass could not
attribute register-based accesses, only absolute operands.

The cartridge is unchanged: the rebuild still differs from the dump in the 42
header bytes of modification M1 and nowhere else, and tests/run_all.sh ends
with ALL CHECKS PASSED after scripts/regenerate.sh regenerated the split,
symbols and sources from this table.
