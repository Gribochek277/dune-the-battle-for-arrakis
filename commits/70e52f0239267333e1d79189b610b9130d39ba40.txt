ticket 140: GAMEPAD MAPPING is the template's second port screen
Ticket 139 projected the front end's binding list onto the settings screen's
engine from `settings_screen.c` and called it a placeholder. This is the screen
it becomes: `next/gamepad_mapping.c` is a `next_menu_screen` of its own, built
from the front end's registered rows, with its title, its window (the screen it
was opened from), the cartridge's move sound, its status line, the capture entry
on A, the cancel on B and its own way out. The picture, the setup's guest calls,
the drawing, the pad's edge, the pointer, the frame's plan and the sound layer
stay in `settings_screen.c`; the settings screen's first row pushes the mapping
screen over itself and its way out pops back. The two screens now differ by their
content, not their machinery.

The template gained two capabilities, each one field: `next_menu_row.literal` /
`value_literal`, so a row can carry the binding file's own words by pointer (an
edit's in-place rebuild reaches the screen on the next frame with no second
projection), and `next_menu_screen.status`, the line under the window, asked on
every frame the screen is up. PORT SETTINGS leaves both empty, so its descriptor
needed no change beyond the two NULLs.

The move sound is the descriptor's and the guest's layer's: `settings_screen.c`
submits it through `SubmitSoundIfEnabled` ($018B90) from the cycle, exactly as the
guest's options loop does, and the frame's plan resumes when the driver returns.
The mapping descriptor names 25; the settings descriptor keeps naming 0, which is
the silent control the new sound test uses.

Kept, not re-litigated: the capture is an edit and not a move (A on a capturable
row is consumed before the engine sees it; a refusal falls through to the row's
choose, ticket 112), B cancels a waiting capture, the control is withheld until it
is released (app/app.c, unchanged), and the way out still tells the front end the
list was put away before it pops.

Tests: `port/tests/test_gamepad_mapping.c` is the unit half (the rows by pointer,
the engine's walk, the move sound, the capture and its cancel, the fall-through,
the way out) -- 301 checks; `tests/test_port_settings_screen.sh` adds the cursor's
slide ($C0 -> $C4 -> $C8 -> $CC -> $D0 frame by frame), the move sound's YM2612
trace with the silent settings screen as its control, and the cleared status line
on the way back, and its reviewed picture is unchanged (7271f68d...). Verified:
scripts/build.sh, make -C port test, tests/test_port_settings_screen.sh,
