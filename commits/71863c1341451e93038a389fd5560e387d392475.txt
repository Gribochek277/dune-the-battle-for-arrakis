D154: one picture per refresh of the display, its alpha the instant it stands for
The picture was drawn on a 60.00 Hz grid of the run's own while the swap waited
for the display's vblank -- two clocks -- and its alpha was the wall clock's at
the moment the loop happened to draw, after whatever cartridge frame ran first.
Measured (cc0f32ec): every 900-frame run at 2560x1440 and 1920x1080 had the
nearest cartridge frame repeat and skip 50-170 times and content steps off by
10-60/256 of a frame on average, up to 350.

The picture now has one clock, the display's (app/pacing.h, "The picture's grid"):

* platform: Windows' compositor names the period and a vblank
  (mdp_window_refresh(), asked once per picture); a slot is 2 ms after each
  vblank, so a picture has nearly a refresh to be drawn and handed over before
  the vblank that shows it.
* swaps: no platform to ask -- a FIFO present that blocks returns on vblanks,
  and a phase-locked loop follows the returns from SDL's integer rate to the
  display's own (59.94, 143.98); a probe of back-to-back presents fills a queue
  that only blocks when full; a present that never blocks leaves the nominal
  grid, and one whose 'returns' walk the period off by 2% was not a display.
* rate: --present-rate keeps its meaning, a wall-clock grid from the start.

A cartridge frame and a picture are taken in the order of their instants
(mda_pacing_next(), app.c's dispatch): a frame due after the picture's instant
waits until it is drawn, so the pair always holds the instant and the alpha is
the slot's -- never clamped, never the clock's at the draw. The cartridge's
deadlines are untouched: no frame runs early, none is slowed or sped by a
picture, MDA_PACING_MAX_LATE still gives up lost time.

Measured with the same instrument, rounds interleaved on the owner's host: the
content step error falls to 0.8-7/256 mean and the repeated/skipped frames to
15-51 a run where the presenter keeps up (D154's table). A variant that gave
up a slot after a present returned late was measured and not kept: no
consistent gain, and fewer pictures under load.

MDP_WINDOW_TEST_NO_REFRESH puts a Windows host on the swaps lock.
test_present.c: the grids, the order, the skip, the lock against simulated
presenters (waits for the display / a queue of two / never blocks / costs a
refresh) and the whole loop simulated at 60, 59.94, 120 and 50 Hz.
