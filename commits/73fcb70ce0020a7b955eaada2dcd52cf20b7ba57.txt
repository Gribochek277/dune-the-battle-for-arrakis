next: the pointer's target and drag box reach the wide viewport (D156)
The wide world is drawn for the whole viewport, so a unit can be drawn in
the extra columns left of the cartridge's screen or on the lines above and
below it. next_mission_selection_pointer_at() still refused every line
outside the cartridge's 224, and scan_box() clipped the drag box to the
320x224 screen, so a click or a box there named nothing.

Both now use next/mission_wide.h's viewport. The columns run from -extra to
319. The lines run from hidden_top() to screen_bottom(), which are the
screen's 0..223 outside the wide world, so 4:3 is unchanged. A selftest
fixture shows both cases: the 4:3 picture refuses the band and the border
line, and the wide viewport accepts them.
