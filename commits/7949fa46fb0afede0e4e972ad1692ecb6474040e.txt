ticket 154: the machine's snapshot, and a run that restores to the same picture
A bug that happens after five minutes of scrolling cannot be described in words.
The first half of the player's answer is a state a run can be started from, and
the first thing such a state must do is not lie: a state that restores to a
different picture is worse than no state at all.

`app/state_file.h` is that state, and it is the whole machine: the 68000's
registers, PC, SR and USP; the 64 KiB of work RAM; the VDP's V-RAM, C-RAM,
V-SRAM, its 24 registers, the address register and command latch, the VBlank
flag and frame counter, the pending DMA's busy window and the data-port FIFO's
four slots; the Z80's registers, flags, interrupt latches, bank register, both
YM2612 address latches, the bus handshake and reset line, its own clock and the
machine clock at its last run, and the 8 KiB of sound RAM; the machine's cycle
counter with the DRAM refresh phase; the three controller ports' latches and the
six-button counter; the PSG's chip struct and the YM2612's register file; and the
port's own live state -- the arrangement and the C replacements it ran, the
renderer's name, the display settings and the plan they resolved, and the
mission's pointer, selection set, drag box and pending order. What it leaves out
is in the header: sound in flight, the traces a run happened to be writing, the
idle diagnostic and the port screens' transient row state.

The file is a binary blob with a text manifest beside it: the magic "MD68KSNP",
a version, the total length, four sections ("MCOR", "Z80S", "SOUN", "PORT") and
a SHA-256 trailer. Every load verifies all of it -- and the run's identity: the
engine, the --replace names, the renderer and the cartridge's SHA-256 are
checked, never adopted (the arrangement is assembled before the state is read,
and a state that silently changed which routines run as C would be the lie this
exists to prevent) -- before it touches the machine, so a truncated, edited or
foreign file is a refusal with its reason and the prepared machine is untouched.

`--debug` writes a session directory (`run.log` with one digest line per guest
frame, `state-<n>.bin` and `frame-<n>.png` every N frames or every frame on
request, `manifest.txt`), `--debug-load PATH` starts a run from a state, and the
harness refuses `--debug` loudly with its own three switches: `--state-save
FRAME:PATH`, `--state-load PATH` and `--state-log PATH` -- the same format, so
the acceptance is a test with no window.

The machine accessors the restore needed, each documented where it lives:
mdz80_state_get/set (the whole Z80 and its two clocks), md68k_cycles_restore with
md68k_cycles_refresh_phase, mdloop_seat (the frame clock's grid from the restored
point), md68k_pad_state/set_state, md68k_ym2612_registers_get/set (replayed
through the chip's own write path), md68k_sound_seat (the timeline moved, not
played, so a restore does not render a minute of unheard audio), next_app_seat
and mda_machine_seat, and a get/set pair for the four next/ modules that keep
the port's own state.

The first version round-tripped for three frames and then drifted by ten cycles
with the Z80 a few instructions out of phase: the DRAM refresh's phase is state
too, and restoring the total without it moves every later charge. Measured on
the round trip rather than assumed.

Tests: `tests/test_state_file.sh` in the fast tier (a state at frame 1 000 of a
1 060-frame run, 60 frames from it compared line for line -- RAM, the VDP's
memories, the picture, every 68000 register and flag, the Z80, the cycle clock --
and the exit PNGs byte for byte; the comparison proved able to fail with a state
from the wrong frame; the refusals with their messages and no output; the
window's session and its resume under SDL's dummy driver). `port/tests/test_state.c`
in `make -C port test` is the field-by-field half: values a guest would take
thousands of frames to reach (a selection set, a drag box, a pending order, a
pad mid-handshake, a stopped Z80) planted, saved, cleared and required back,
with a sentinel proving every refused load wrote nothing. Verified by hand on a
mission run too (8 100 frames of `atriedes-mission.pad`, a state at 8 000, 100
frames identical) and on `--engine next` with the whole replacement table.

Docs: `docs/debugging.md` (the new page: the flags, the format, the identity
rule, the cadences and their cost, what proves it, what it is not), D70,
`docs/build-and-test.md`, `port/README.md`, the zone declaration and the
regenerated site. scripts/build.sh, make -C port test and tests/run_all.sh are
green.
