next: the mission map view's frame path is the port's C
The map view is a 64x64 grid of MapCells shown through one record at
$FFFFBF12, and this takes the half of it that runs every frame over:
InitMissionView $0024CC, SetMapScrollDelta $00251A, SetMapScrollPosition
$002522 (with UpdateMapViewEntry $00256E and its off-map case), SetMapScrollLimits
$002706, RedrawMapPlane $002882, UploadMapRow $0028EE, PackMapRow $002B3E and
UpdateMapViewport $00297C (with the draw script's tail at $0029AE). One
implementation, next_mission_map_view(), is eight rows because each routine is
its own entry in the guest's image and its own call site; the files are one
region each (the scroll records, the plane's rows, the per-frame step), one case
per guest instruction charged at its own address.

The record, measured. $FFFFBF12 is MapScrollPosition: word +$0 is the view's x
and +$2 its y -- the pass-3 evidence's "row in the high word, column in the low"
is wrong (UpdateCursorPosition $002BD2 adds the high word to the screen x and
$002BE4 the low one to the y), and the curated line is corrected with this
ticket. +$4/+$6 are the sprite attributes and index LoadViewportDescriptor
writes; +$8/+$C are the two clamp longs SetMapScrollLimits stores. The
arguments of SetMapScrollPosition are chunk indices: each changed word selects
an entry of data_08424A and stores its first long -- the chunk's tilemap source
-- in $FFFFBF04/$FFFFBF08, where the cursor's edge scroll reads and clears it.
A negative index is the off state ($002590): fixed descriptor data_083F72,
priority bits cleared, delta not applied. The map's limits have two writers and
the placement mode wins: $00C20C passes $00180018/$012E00CE, $0180DC overwrites
it with $00180018/$012800C8, which is what the recording carries.
