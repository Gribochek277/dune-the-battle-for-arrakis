204: the un-sliced leaves are the port's C
Ticket 204 takes the twenty-four routines of $000218-$0015AA that had no group:
the error and `rte` stubs, the map cell's tile class and its shared tail, the
fixed-point multiply and the two approximate distances, the seeded random pair,
the VRAM/VSRAM clears, the backdrop and the two palette fades, the VSRAM scroll
write and the sprite-record library. They become one group, `un-sliced-leaves`,
paired, with zero delegations (191 rows in the table, 24 in the group).

The group has two shapes, and each one is a measurement:

* Eleven whole routines (span 0) -- the leaf pattern of ticket 182: one dispatch
  runs the guest's routine to its `rts`, every instruction charged at its own
  address, the leaf calls inlined (RandomInRange's RandomByte, sub_0009CE's
  ApproxDistance).
* Thirteen transcribed spans -- a VRAM fill's DMA trigger and status polls, the
  VSRAM data-port writes' FIFO slots, the fades' per-step VBlank waits and the
  four entry points into those bodies, sub_000F80's `dbf` copy (over 200 000
  guest instructions in one recorded dispatch: a frame's work), and the three
  sprite-record routines that mask the interrupt with `move #$2700,sr`, whose
  handler the guest delivers *inside* the routine (the YM timeline caught it at
  frame 1451: two timer registers stamped a frame later).

Charging is the timeline, not the total: the first version summed
md68k_instruction_cost() like the leaf helpers, the cycle count stayed right and
the sampled sound drifted by a few LSB across 8 442 samples in 402 runs where
ApproxDistance is called. Every instruction now goes through
next_replacement_charge(), so the Z80 advances at the guest's instruction
boundaries, and the mission pair's samples are identical.

Proof: tests/test_un_sliced_leaves.sh (22 committed fixtures, each replayed
against the C, the live guest and the fuzz; the 9 500-frame mission pair
identical in picture/RAM/cycles/YM/samples/audio with the instruction count
lower by exactly the 104 100 the C reports; the group-off control byte for byte;
the 2 700-frame house-selection pair identical). The 10 400-frame playtest route
is identical except its instruction count (123 798 fewer). ErrorStub and
data_000220 have no fixture and are declared in tests/test_oracle.sh: the first
is the cartridge's own infinite loop, the second is the shared `rte` whose
recorded states agree but whose exception frame the fuzz mutates into a state the
mechanism's interrupt-frame guard reads as a call's.

The group's C is four files (the zone rule's 64 KiB bound): the whole routines,
the clears, the records' spans and a shared header. D115 records the decisions;
the D91 table has the group's row; the inventory, the guest boundary and the site
are regenerated.
