message:
machine: the Z80 has a stand-in for while it executes nothing
With the port's audio engine as the sound path the Z80 runs nothing, and
mdz80_run() levelled its clock and returned: whatever the driver would have
cost the 68000 was gone. mdz80_set_stand_in() gives that place a hook. It is
called where the Z80 would have run -- after every 68000 instruction -- with
the machine's clock and whether the Z80 would have been stopped (the 68000
holds its bus, or the reset line is asserted), and the machine charges one
md68k_cycles_z80_bus() for every access it reports due, asking again until
none is, because the charge moves the clock the way BlastEm's
z80_read_bank() moves it.

NULL is the default and charges nothing, so this commit changes no run; the
comparison build's Z80 executes and never consults it (D150).
