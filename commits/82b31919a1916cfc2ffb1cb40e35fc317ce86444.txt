transpiler: translate the last 15 fallbacks as illegal-opcode faults
The fifteen instructions ticket 45 left behind were not emitter gaps.
Checked against the manual and two decoders: ADDQ/SUBQ with an
address-register destination is defined for word and long only, and
`bchg d5,#$FF` uses the immediate operand, which no bit operation
accepts. The interpreter's decoder rejects both, BlastEm's
m68k_decode() returns M68K_INVALID for both, and none of the fifteen
addresses appears in any coverage log -- they are inline data that the
disassembler decoded as code.

Performing the operation would put the translated path at odds with the
interpreter that diff_sweep compares every case against, so
Emitter.illegal_encoding() emits exactly what the decoder does:
md68k_next() for the instruction length, then
md68k_fault(MD68K_FAULT_OPCODE, addr, op). The address, opcode word and
pc after the fault are therefore identical on both paths, immediate
word included. The bit-op emitter mirrors the decoder's other
rejection too: a modifying form with a PC-relative destination would
need a store the 68000 never performs.

scripts/transpile68k.py now reports 39 593 of 39 593 (100.000 %),
0 fallbacks and 0 duplicates; the duplicate guard and the printed share
are unchanged. MD68K_FALLBACK stays in the runtime for addresses the
switch has no case for, which this cartridge no longer uses.

Coverage in make -C port test: three rows in test_translated.c naming
the real cartridge encodings ($01F5D2, $01FEE8, $01F73E) with the
hand-computed expectation of a fault and untouched state, and three
hand-assembled dual-path fixtures in tests/fixtures/fixtures.asm whose
harness now compares the recorded faults of the translated and
interpreted runs. test_boot.c's $01F73E fixture asserts that the fault
comes from the generated case instead of the fallback. The full-ROM
sweep still agrees on all 39 593 addresses.

Docs: native-port.md (coverage table, evidence table, a ticket 48
section explaining why these encodings are faults), decisions.md D20
superseding D19, port/README.md.
