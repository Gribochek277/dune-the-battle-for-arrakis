PORT SETTINGS is a descriptor on the menu template, and its content only
Ticket 138 extracted the shape every menu in the port has into next_menu_screen
(a descriptor) and next_menu_engine (the walk), and proved it on the cartridge's
OPTIONS. This moves the port's own settings screen onto it, which is the ticket's
point: a port screen can be content and nothing else.

The rows are one descriptor: GAMEPAD MAPPING (the screen's own code, opening the
front end's list), the POINTER row (ticket 142: a two-state value, PORT ARROW or
SYSTEM, read and written through next_settings_ops and app/settings.c) and BACK
TO TITLE (an EXIT with the screen's result). Their words, bands, values, masks
and actions are the entries; the navigation, the cursor and its slide, the
window, the wrap and the two sound decisions are the engine's, unchanged from
the OPTIONS proof. GAMEPAD MAPPING's list, still the front end's until ticket
140, is projected onto the same engine when the row opens it, so the cursor, the
window and the ticket 117 line are the code the screen's own rows use, not a
second loop.

The template gained one thing: a value can name its states' words. A port
screen's values are locale keys and a translated screen has to say them, so
next_menu_value carries `words` and next_menu_value_text() answers the word of
the state the engine would cycle, through the row's own read. The guest's
OPTIONS leaves it NULL (its values are the cartridge's strings, uploaded by the
guest's own call). That keeps ticket 142's pointer row one entry.

Kept: the capture entry (decided before the engine sees the press, so a capture
is never also a choose, and a refused one still falls through, ticket 112), the
leave, the wrap at both ends, the ticket 117 line and every existing check --
tests/test_port_settings_screen.sh (picture, rows, cursor sprite at both ends of
a scrolled window), tests/test_mouse.sh (the pointer, the POINTER row's two
values, and the pinned settings and mapping pictures byte-identical to the
reviewed ones) and tests/test_binding_flow.py. Changed on purpose: the cursor
now slides four pixels a frame (ticket 137), a press during a slide is dropped,
and the way out runs one more frame because the exit row's action, the frame and
the leave are the engine's order.

next/port_window.{c,h} and its test are deleted: the menu engine answers the
window now (next_menu_window_top) and two computations of one window is the
duplication the template exists to end. D56 records the decision; the docs site,
the zone model and the module table are rebuilt.

Evidence: port/tests/test_settings_screen.c (the new unit half -- the
descriptor, the engine's walk, the rows' actions, the pointer row's values
through a stubbed seam, and a fourth row added as one entry),
port/tests/test_row_lists.c (the rows as data, including the value's two words)
and port/tests/test_menu_screen.c (next_menu_value_text on the template's
fixture). make -C port test and tests/run_all.sh are green.
