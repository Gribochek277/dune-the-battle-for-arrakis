The mouse's three buttons: left selects, right clears, middle pans
The mission's mouse gets the three-button behaviour a PC RTS has. The left
button selects (what it did), the right one empties the selection set, and the
middle one held down drags the map view the way holding C on the pad does. The
pad's own commit does the moving: the drag asks for no second rule and no
second clamp.

* `app/input_map.{c,h}`: the shipped mouse map binds left = `POINTER_CLICK`,
  right = `POINTER_CLEAR` (new) and middle = `POINTER_DRAG` (new, a held
  level). The vocabulary gains `pointer_click`, `pointer_back`,
  `pointer_clear` and `pointer_drag`, so the mapping screen can show a map
  that binds them and a binding file can spell them; `pointer_back` stays for a
  player who wants the old route. `mda_input` gains `clear` (an edge) and
  `drag` (a level), and the loop hands both to the front end's `on_pointer`.
* `next/mission_selection.{c,h}`: the right button empties the set, the
  primary and the guest's own `SelectedRecord` `$FFFFC25C` -- the `+$6` bit 15
  marker with them -- and a right click with nothing to clear does nothing at
  all (no write, no generation, so no report line). The middle button's
  movement becomes the guest's own direction bits (a four-pixel threshold per
  poll) and the frame goes to the mission cursor with the held level.
* `next/mission_pointer.{c,h}`: the frame's drag direction and held level. The
  release frame is still the pointer's, with no direction, because the mission
  handler's idle entry coasts in the previous pad `$FFFFBF48`: the release is
  what lets the cursor's C forget it, so the view stops where the drag left it.
  The position is not written while the drag is -- the view moves and the
  cursor rides it.
* `next/mission_cursor_pad.c`, `next/mission_cursor_placement.c`: at
  `$003118`/`$003AD0` the drag puts its nibble in `d0` and the pad's C bit
  (bit 5 of the guest's own pad layout) in both halves of `d5`, so the
  cartridge's sixteen-case table, its commit at `$0032A0`/`$003BB4`, its
  whole-cell branch and its `$FFFFE3D2`-`$FFFFE3D6` and `$18`/`$128` clamps
  move the view -- one rule, one clamp.
* `app/run_play.c`: the port's screens get `back || clear`, so the right button
  keeps the panels' way back that ticket 145 shipped; the mission map gets the
  two edges apart. The deliberate divergence (D60) is the clear itself and the
  drag's stopped release: `legacy` has no pointer and no selection, and the
  mission-cursor differential still compares the same bytes.
* `port/tests/test_replacement.c`: the port's C with the drag against the
  guest's own pad word `C|RIGHT`, from one constructed state: every register,
  flag, the four speed bytes, the 64 KiB of work RAM minus the four
  `ReadController` pad bytes (the drag asks for no pad button, so those are the
  no-pad run's zeroes), the VDP counters, the clock (14 286) and the view
  record. `port/tests/test_selection.c`: the clear (the set, the guest's record
  and the marker; the generation does not move when there is nothing to clear)
  and the drag's direction bits, threshold and release frame.
  `port/tests/test_input_map.c`: the three shipped rows, the clear edge, the
  drag level and the four names in the vocabulary.
* `tests/test_mission_mouse.sh`: nine window runs on the Atreides route. A left
  click selects unit 38 and a right click clears it (`$FFFFC25C` 0, the marker
  clear, the report `count=0` after the click's `count=1`); a right click with
  nothing selected prints no selection line at all; a middle drag walks
  `$FFFFE3BE` from the mission's 647 to the guest's own limit `$FFFFE3D2` (512)
  and leaves the cursor riding the view (190, not the hand's 20); and after a
  ten-poll drag's release the view is 717 at 7 790 and the same at 7 820 while
  the hand's next move takes the cursor to 260. Every feature run's
  `--record-input` is the replay's own button lines.
