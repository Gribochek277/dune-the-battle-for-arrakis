docs: what the port matches and what the harness found
docs/native-port.md gains a "Differential harness" section: the exact commands,
the frame clock defined in guest terms, the result (frame 1 matches the
reference byte for byte: all 65536 bytes of work RAM, every register, the same
interrupted PC and stack pointer), the first divergence (frame 2, $00103C: the
byte/word move N-flag emitter defect sends `bpl.s $00105E` at $001042 down the
wrong path, so the graphics decompressor at $000F8C is skipped and the scratch
buffer the reference leaves at $FF0000+ is missing -- ticket 44 owns that fix),
and the reason frames 3+ are unreachable (the game waits for the Z80 driver at
$0D52EA with the interrupt mask at 7, milestone N5).

It also records the two interpreter defects the harness itself found and fixed
in the previous commit, together with the fixtures that pin them, and what the
comparison deliberately does not cover (z80_ram, and the address logs, which
have different block granularities on the two sides).

docs/build-and-test.md documents the comparison command next to the reference
capture and lists tests/test_headless.sh in the checks table.
