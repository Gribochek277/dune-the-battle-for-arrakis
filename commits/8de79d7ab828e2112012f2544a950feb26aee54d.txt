YM2612: the FM half of the machine, so the game's music exists
port/runtime/ym2612.c/.h is the chip: six four-operator FM channels with
all eight algorithms and their feedback paths, the envelope generators
(attack/decay/sustain/release, key scaling, SSG-EG), the LFO's PM and AM,
the two timers and the status byte, channel 6's DAC mode and the pan
registers, at BlastEm's own level convention. Its log-sine and power
tables are generated by scripts/make_ym2612_tables.py, whose --verify
compares all 8704 entries with BlastEm's own; the write paths of both
CPUs now apply their bytes to the chip (mdz80.c) and sound.c's mixer adds
its output to the PSG's at the chip's own 1008-master-clock sample grid.

Measured, not asserted:

* the synthesis against BlastEm's own ym2612.c core, on a shared
  4000-write program over every register: 64706 samples, maximum
  difference 0 (a development-time probe, recorded in the docs);
* the register stream against the reference's: the pinned emulator's own
  VGM log of the house-selection recording and the port's --ym-log
  timeline are the same 500 writes in the same order, frame alignment
  within 1.31 frames and drifting 0.10 (scripts/capture_ym_log.py,
  scripts/compare_ym_logs.py, tests/test_ym_log.sh);
* the audio against the reference's recording: tests/test_audio_compare.sh
  now passes at the thresholds this ticket fixes after measuring both
  sides -- 3 dB per 0.25 s window, 50 cents of pitch, RMS-envelope
  correlation >= 0.6 -- with r = 0.805 and the music's notes within
  2..13 cents. tests/test_audio.sh measures the programmed FM tone from
  an independent implementation (203.20 Hz, F-NUM 500 at block 4).

port/tests/test_ym2612.c adds the arithmetic fixtures (36173 checks in
the suite now): the tables' notable values, the F-NUM/block/multiple
phase arithmetic, the envelope's four phases with key on/off, the
algorithms and feedback, the LFO's PM step, the DAC, the timers and the
status byte's BUSY window and latched port, both CPUs' four-port write
paths, and the rendering (the measured frequency, the pan/level
convention, the WAV). The status byte is driver-visible -- the driver
busy-waits on it -- so tests/test_recording.sh accepts one frame-clock
stack byte at the checkpoint, with its own limit and the reason written
down. What is still not modelled is listed in ym2612.h and
docs/native-port.md.

Nothing under src/ or disassembly/ changed; tests/run_all.sh ends with
ALL CHECKS PASSED.
