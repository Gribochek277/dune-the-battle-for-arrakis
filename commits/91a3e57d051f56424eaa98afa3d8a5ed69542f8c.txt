The transcription is generated. The seven rows are 3 100 guest instructions and
every body is the generated translation's own text with its terminator turned
into a goto, a `call_guest()` or a `BOUNDARY()`; ticket 187 wrote that shape by
hand and at this size the diff is unreadable. `scripts/transcribe_group.py`
writes it from `port/generated/`: one label per instruction, `STEP` charged at
its own address, the guest's branches as the C's branches, a call as a return
address pushed and `pc` left at the callee, a `jmp` as `pc` set, a `dc.w` word as
the machine's interpreter (D41), and a PC-relative operand resolved to the
absolute address it names (the part does not advance `pc` between instructions).
The manifest of what each part owns is a literal in the script, so a range is a
diff and `--check` is a gate (tests/test_structures.sh runs it). It refuses a
shape it does not recognise, which is how the `divu`-by-zero guard and the `dbf`
two-branch body were caught rather than mistranscribed. Eleven part files keep
every module under the zone rule's 64 KiB bound.

Three rules the ported code showed the rules page had wrong, corrected in the
same change and named there:

  - E3: a build's construction phase is TEN payments, not twenty. `+$55` starts
    at 100 ($0183D2) and `subi.b #$0A,$55` takes ten off each payment, so ten
    payments of `+$16/20` (divs #$14 at $006E82) finish it -- half the type's
    cost. The other half is the repair path's per-hit-point price, which is why
    the divisor is twenty and not ten. Whether the two add up to exactly `+$16`
    is left as the observation that would settle it.
  - E1: the income clamp is max(side +$16, $FFFFC054) ($007F2A-$007F3E), not the
    side's `+$16` alone. The committed route's player has `+$16` = 0 and is still
    capped at the 1 000 in `$FFFFC054`, which the page quoted without explaining.
  - D4: the damage mask `$FFFFC019` bit 0 protects structures as well as units
    ($009C56-$009C6A is the same test as $01C558, read as a byte). The page named
    only the unit half.
