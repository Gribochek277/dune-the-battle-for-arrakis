port: fix two interpreter ALU decoding defects
The differential harness (next commit) found both of these on its first run:
with them, the port faulted at $FFFFFF after 128 254 instructions, in the
middle of the boot.

* SUB and CMP computed their operands the wrong way round in both directions:
  `sub.w a0,d0` ($9048) ran as A0 - D0 instead of D0 - A0, and `cmp.w a1,d1`
  set the flags of A1 - D1. The boot's work-RAM guard at $000B08 is that
  instruction, so the guard ran past the end of RAM and overwrote its own
  return address. BlastEm's answer for $9048 was checked through its GDB stub
  (D0 = D0 - A0) and is what the fix follows.

* `cmp.w a1,d1` ($B249) was decoded as `cmpm (a1)+,(a1)+`: the decoder took
  "bits 5-3 = 001" alone as the CMPM marker, but CMPM also needs opmode 1xx.
  Every compare against an address register therefore post-incremented that
  register, which broke the free-memory list build at $001518.

Both are pinned by new fixtures in port/tests/test_isa.c with hand-computed
expectations (the reversed answers differ from the expected ones, so the
fixtures can fail).
