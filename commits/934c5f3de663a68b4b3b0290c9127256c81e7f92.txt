AGENTS.md: 'if you touch it, it becomes C' is a rule, not a preference
The owner asked for this to be internalised rather than remembered, so it goes
where every session reads it. The old wording said 'prefer C to the listing',
which leaves room to prefer something else on a busy day; the rule has a trigger
and a consequence instead: a routine, screen or behaviour you changed does not
stay in the generated listing or in assembly, and does not grow a special case
there.

The boundary is stated with it, because the same files hold the evidence that the
port runs the cartridge and that work must not move: the transcriptions are proof,
and proof is not a place to live. Byte-exactness still applies while the C stands
in for the guest's routine and stops applying the moment the C does something the
cartridge never did (D36), which is the case the last few tickets kept having to
re-derive.
