The mission's layers, measured: world and interface, and the rule the wide view uses
Ticket 134 measures what the VDP draws at the three mission checkpoints (7 898,
8 148, 9 498), assigns every layer and every sprite class to the world or the
interface, and records the layout rule ticket 132 implements.

The measurement composes the four picture layers with the reference renderer's
rules and reproduces each checkpoint's picture exactly (0 of 71 680 pixels); the
fixture is analysis/mission-layers.json, written and checked by the new
scripts/mission_layers.py from the reference's VDP dump and 68000 state at the
same VBlank entry.

What it found: plane B ($C000) is the map's terrain and plane A ($E000) its void
and edge dither -- both world, both scrolled by the same hscroll entry; the
window is the interface's own plane and is disabled on the map and the whole
screen on the build panel ($B000, 66 923 of 71 680 pixels); the credits, the
radar square and marker, the structure-icon column, the cursor, the selection
box and the units are sprites, and the build panel's widgets are a second sprite
pool.

The role is not in the VDP state: the sprite attribute table has position, size,
tile, palette and priority and no owner. The sprite record pool resolves it --
the node's dest is a unit record, the mission's view block, a constant ROM
position or the panel's record -- and the node's +8 descriptor names the class.
The check's controls prove it: without the state every entry is reported
unclassified and the pool is named; repointing the cursor's node at a unit
record turns the same two entries into the world; clearing the panel's window
registers empties the window layer; a changed sprite tile stops the pool from
reproducing the table.

The decision (D42): the scene stays a pure VDP view. The layer map is the
mission's data, read by the mission's C for the wide view, not a scene field --
the VDP state cannot carry it, and the scene's record-free contract is what
keeps every backend off the guest's layouts. The renderer seam is untouched and
legacy stays 4:3.

Names added with evidence: RunStructurePanel $017BAC, InitStructurePanel
$00432A, StructurePanelFrame $004264, StructurePanelSpritePool $FFFFF5B0.
