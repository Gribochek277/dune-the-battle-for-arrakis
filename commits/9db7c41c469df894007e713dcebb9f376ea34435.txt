ticket 104: the sprite lands and tracks the rows but draws nothing -- the shadow is 68000-side, the renderer wants VDP-side
With a byte destination ($F000, per 106) the entry reaches the table the renderer reads
and follows state.cursor exactly: row 0 -> Y 32, row 1 -> Y 40. A render with the sprite
against one without differs in zero pixels.

render_software.c's own entry description explains it: +0 Y biased by $80, +2 size/link,
+4 tile id and attributes, +6 X biased by $80. That is the VDP-side layout.
SpriteTableShadow is the 68000-side one -- +0 Y, +2 pattern/attr, +4 X, +6 unused --
which is what BuildSpriteTable fills and what the game's upload converts on the way to
V-RAM. Copying it straight across puts X where the renderer reads the tile id, and an
unbiased Y where it subtracts $80: the game's Y=288 renders at row 160, exactly where the
title-screen arrow appears, while my Y=32 rendered at -96.

Three things settled: the port's V-RAM write is correct and verified; the conversion is
the missing step and it is small and fully specified by the renderer's own comment; and
writing the entry directly is clearly the right shape -- the port would be doing the same
conversion, not imitating the routine.

One thing still open, and it is the last one: the cursor's tile id. The shadow's pattern
word is 0x0000, so it does not come from there -- the upload takes it from the 12-byte
pattern descriptor at node+8, whose decoding this ticket already declined to guess at.
One small structure, named rather than assumed.

Reverted; main green. Closest the cursor has ever been, and every remaining gap is named.
