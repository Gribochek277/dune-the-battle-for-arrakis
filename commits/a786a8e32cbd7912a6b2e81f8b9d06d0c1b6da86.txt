message:
the overlay layer tracks its writes: a dragged box no longer smears
The owner's playtest of ticket 230's first build: dragging the selection box left
every intermediate rectangle on the screen. The layer had the machinery for it --
`begin()` clears the previous picture's rectangles and the upload carries them --
but nothing told it what was drawn: `app/pointer.c`, `app/selection_overlay.c` and
`app/backdrop.c` draw into the layer's `md_draw_target` and describe nothing, so
`previous` stayed empty, nothing was erased, and the empty upload list made the
presenter send the *whole* layer, now holding every box the drag had drawn.

`md_draw_target` grew an opt-in bounds tracker (one comparison per write; the
picture's own target leaves it off) and the layer turns it on: `begin()` starts the
box empty, a picture's writes accumulate it, and `end()` marks it as the current
picture's rectangle. An explicit `md_overlay_layer_mark()` is still accepted and
just makes the upload tighter; a caller that marks nothing gets a correct one.

Evidence: `port/tests/test_draw_target.c` (the box holds every write, refused
writes add nothing, a new picture starts empty) and `port/tests/test_overlay.c`
(a shape drawn without a mark is uploaded and erased the next picture) are the new
units, and `tests/test_present_gpu.sh` grew the window case that is the smear
itself: a mouse script walks the port's arrow across sixty frames and the two
presenters must write the same PNG at the end. With the tracking turned off the
case fails, which is the control; the same comparison on the owner's box route
(`--frames 7900`, the mission's drag) is identical now and differed by 7 954
pixels before.
