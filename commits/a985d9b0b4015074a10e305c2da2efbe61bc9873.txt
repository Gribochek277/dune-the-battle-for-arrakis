wiki: the documentation is published to the project's wiki (D158, ticket 229)
The prose moves to the project's Gitea wiki, and `docs/wiki` is a submodule of
the wiki's repository: the same files are the wiki's pages for a reader and the
site's sources for `scripts/build_docs.py`, so the documentation keeps one
history, one review, and the site keeps being generated and gated from it.

`scripts/wiki.py` owns what a person does not:

* `publish` writes the prose with `{{fact:KEY}}` resolved to
  `<value><!--fact:KEY-->` (D159) -- the reader sees the measurement, the marker
  keeps it honest, and `check` fails when a published number stops being the
  measurement's;
* the three links only the repository can resolve are rewritten: `docs/html/` to
  the repository URL (the site is an artifact, Gitea cannot render it), the
  structure diagram to the file the wiki carries beside the page, and the
  `docs/` prefix the root files use;
* `Home` and `_Sidebar` are generated from the same page list, in both
  languages once a translation exists;
* `check` is the gate's half: pages current, markers true, every Ukrainian page
  paired with its English one and linked both ways.

Naming (`scripts/wiki_names.py`): Gitea's wiki is flat -- its routes do not
support subdirectories -- and a page name may not carry a space or a
parenthesis, because both renderers end a link destination there. So a page is
the repository's own file stem and the reader sees "build and test" for
`build-and-test.md`; a translation is `<name>-UKR`, not `<name> (UKR)`.

`scripts/install-tools.sh` initialises the submodule: a fresh worktree has no
pages without it, and `tests/test_docs.sh` cannot build the site.
