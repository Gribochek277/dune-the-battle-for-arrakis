audio engine: the music tick counts from the driver's restart, not from command 22
The engine cleared its tempo accumulator ($0887) and step ($0885) at command 22
and ticked on every VBlank. The driver does neither: `$0AB9` (command 22) clears
the channels and calls `$0CD2` and leaves the accumulator alone, which counts
from the driver's own power-on -- the restart LoadSoundDriver's upload ends
with -- and the VBlank the 68000 takes between that upload and its reset never
reaches the Z80 (the reset drops the pending interrupt). So the engine's music
ticks were the driver's on only 1 248 of the driver's 1 922 over the playtest
route's first 3 000 frames; the accumulator now restarts at
next_audio_engine_select_set_from_tables() (LoadSoundDriver's entry) and a
frame taken while the Z80's bus is held or its reset asserted is not a tick,
and the two agree on 1 920.

This moves the engine's notes onto the cartridge's frames, so what the played
audio does and when changes with it; the audio gates hold
(tests/test_audio_engine.sh, tests/test_audio_modern.sh,
tests/test_sound_events.sh). It is what D150's driver bus model needs: the
model's copies follow the engine's ticks.

port/tests/audio_engine_main.c's engine mode now releases the Z80's reset
line, because the machine comes up with it held and the engine treats a held
reset as no driver to hear the VBlank.
