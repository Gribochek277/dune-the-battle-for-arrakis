ticket 100: the handoff is a cycle over addresses the port already owns -- no front end takeover
md68k_jsr pushes md68k_next_address(cpu, cpu->pc): the return address is the next
guest address, not something the caller picks. That is what makes the port's screen a
two-address cycle inside RunTitleScreen's own span -- step at $00D5A4, jsr WaitVBlank,
continue at $00D5A6, back to $00D5A4 -- and it retires the reason this ticket looked
blocked.

WaitVBlank is guest code, so the machine advances every frame: the frame handler runs,
the music plays, nothing is held, and ticket 85's ~75 underruns per 120 frames stay
retired. The title menu's drawing is never reached because the port took over at
$00D5A4 and never returns to the menu's loop, so there is no second screen to fight
over the plane. Closing is the mirror: perform the rts at $00D5AE with the choice the
cartridge would have left, and RunFrontEnd re-enters the title screen normally.

RunFrontEnd stays guest code and out of the table, so the equivalence gate compares
what it compares today -- which is the whole reason to prefer this to taking the front
end over.
