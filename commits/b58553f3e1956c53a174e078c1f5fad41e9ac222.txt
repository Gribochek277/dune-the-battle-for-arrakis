D145: the mission's world fills the target, and the HUD has a frame of its own
A review found the credits counter above the top edge of a 2560x1080 window.
Ticket 159 raised the world's scale to ceil(W / 447) so the picture is the
target's width, and D77 drew the HUD at that same scale, anchored to the
world's composition -- which is taller than the target whenever the raised
scale cuts the world: at 2560x1080 the world is 6x, 1440 lines in 1080, and
the credits landed at y = -30 (at 1120x480 they were off the top too).

The owner's direction: while a mission is played the world takes every pixel,
and the HUD adapts to the screen without shrinking. So:

* the world's scale is max(wish, ceil(W / 447), ceil(H / 240)): it fills the
  target on both axes (16:10 no longer gets bars above and below), and what is
  taller is centred and cut;
* the HUD's scale is min(world, fit), in a frame of its own: the cartridge's
  320x240 at that scale, held against the target's right edge and centred on
  its height (md_layout_interface_frame());
* the layers split the sprite list instead of the screen: a sprite with the
  priority bit that starts on a region's measured tiles and touches its
  rectangle is the HUD's (md_scene_sprite_is_interface()); the world layer
  draws every other sprite, the HUD layer draws its own over the world. The CPU,
  GL and Vulkan backends take the same decision, and scene-gl-test and
  scene-vk-test hold them to the CPU byte for byte;
* the pointer reads what the target shows: a click on the HUD's frame is the
  HUD's pixel, not the map under its old world-scale rectangle.

1920x1200 (the owner's display) is the picture it was. D77's per-pixel clip of
the world inside the HUD's source rectangles was only right while the two
placements coincided -- with the HUD elsewhere a unit under that rectangle
would vanish -- and it also compared an active line with a picture row (nine
lines apart) on the CPU but not on the GPU; the per-sprite rule replaces it.

Edited, not deleted (D31/D36), because they asserted D77's single scale: the
interface layer fixture in port/tests/test_scene.c (now with a HUD sprite and a
control without it), the frame's interface region in test_frame.c, the region
placement and HUD scale in test_mission_wide.c, the 4X/1X plan that is
buildable now in test_display_layout.c, the 1920x1200 and 1280x720 cycles in
test_scale_cycle.c (6x and 4x are wide pictures at 320 columns), the 21:9 line
and the interface section of tests/test_mission_wide.sh, and the 1708x960 and
1920x1200 lines of tests/test_display_settings.sh. Not done: the cartridge's
vertical camera and cursor bounds (D145, "Not done").
