191: the sound driver's 68000 side is the port's C
The last piece of guest logic in the audio path is the 68000 side of the sound
driver: the enable flag and the music id, the command queue, the wrappers the
screens and the unit paths call, and the stubs that put a byte in the driver's
ring. It is one group now -- `sound`, thirty-two rows in
port/next/sound_api.c (the game's layer) and port/next/sound_api_driver.c (the
interface) -- reached through the seam like the other guest-caller groups,
claimed complete and earned solo.

The driver did not move: the Z80 still runs the cartridge's own image, and the
point of the slice is that its input does not move either. On the
house-selection route and the Atreides mission the two arrangements are
identical in every dimension the comparator has -- picture, work RAM, the 68000
cycle count, the WAV and the YM2612 timeline -- with the instruction count
unchanged, and --no-replace is legacy byte for byte.

Every row is a span because the Z80 says so: RequestZ80Bus, SoundPollReady and
BeginSoundCommand poll the bus-acknowledge line, which the machine sets at the
Z80's next instruction boundary -- between two dispatches, never inside one --
and BeginSoundCommand reads the ring index, which moves with the Z80. D95's
per-instruction charge keeps the sound timeline at the guest's cycle points.

What the transcription measured, and the prose it corrected:

* the command queue is one id deep, not three: SubmitSoundCommand refuses the
  append once its count is 1, so the drain's three-id walk is a bound;
* the vector table at $024000 is thirty-one longs, not the twenty-four the
  notes counted; the seven slots this image calls are the rows, and the rest
  are driver modes nothing here selects (eight of them still in seg_data blobs,
  so D41 keeps them out of every row);
* PlayMusic's same-id compare skips the *stop*, not the submit, and
  CurrentMusicId holds the mapped byte rather than the table index;
* EndSoundCommand has no call-shaped entry (every stub jmps to it with
  BeginSoundCommand's saved SR at a7), so the two are one row;
* UploadZ80Program's sub.l a0,d0 at $0D5352 is a dc.w the assembler cannot
  write back, and the C answers it with the machine's interpreter, so the
  interpreted counts stay equal.

The gate is the register timeline and the audio: 32 rows, twenty-six committed
fixtures (analysis/fixtures/sound/ hashes them; scripts/oracle_fixtures.sh
makes them), six declared -- the voice line has no route, the alternate music
device is dead behind $FFFFC017, and three read Z80 state the corpus does not
carry, proved from constructed states in port/tests/test_sound_api.c -- the
pair on two routes, the control, the three cases the ticket names (a music
start at frame 2236, a stop at 2206, sound 41 at 2235 inside the palette fade)
and the playtest route audible. D99 records the decisions and the findings;
docs/game-rules.md §8 is the rule list the C keeps.
