ticket 101: the priority-bit mechanism written next to the rows is corrected
The comment over the settings screen's layout, and the ledger in docs/next-engine.md
that repeated it, said the backdrop's plane A tilemap carries the priority bit in every
cell, that a cell drawn at the game's own rows is hidden by the artwork, and that the
layout is where the tilemap sets no priority. Measured off --vdp-dump, the machine
holds the opposite:

* all 2 048 words of the artwork's own name table ($6000-$6FFF) are clear of the
  priority bit, in the ROM's copy of the resource, in the port's dump and in the game's
  own options dump;
* the port's rows sit over the artwork -- plane A rows 8..16 hold 35-40 non-zero cells
  each -- and read because the rows themselves carry $E000, the same attribute the
  game's own options rows carry;
* the rule that is real runs the other way: plane B loses to plane A wherever plane A
  has a pixel and the priority bits are equal, which render_software.c states as
  (a & VDP_LAYER_PRIORITY) >= (pixel & VDP_LAYER_PRIORITY).

So there was nothing to clear, and the earlier comment's second half described code
that no longer exists. The 27 pixels of $0E0E that code produced were real and stay in
the record as the lesson. Comment-only in the port: no constant, no behaviour and no
picture changed; the site is rebuilt for the markdown.
