D153: Vulkan is the only GPU renderer -- the picture at the cartridge's resolution, scaled once into the window
The window's GPU presenter is Vulkan's (`--presenter gpu`, renderer `vk`, the
default) and OpenGL is gone: output/render_scene_gl*.c, platform/gl_context.*,
the GL window target, `--renderer gl`, scene-gl-test, present-gl-test and
tests/test_scene_gl.py.

The backend is five files behind output/render_scene_vk_private.h: the engine
(the loader's entry points, the instance with the window's surface extensions,
the surface, one queue that draws, computes and presents, at HIGH global
priority where the device offers it), the first pass (a state packed into one
72 KB buffer, uploaded with one copy and drawn once by one compute dispatch at
the cartridge's resolution -- the world up to 447x240 and the HUD's 320x240 --
into a state slot later pictures reuse), the present (one fragment pass a
target pixel: the composition at its integer scales, the blend, the backdrop's
art and the overlay layer read inside its shapes' box), the window's FIFO
swapchain (rebuilt on a resize, fullscreen and an out-of-date present) and the
seam's object. Three frames in flight with their own fences, semaphores and
persistent mapped staging (the overlay's dirty rectangles, D142/D152; the art
once a screen); a frame the device is still drawing is a skipped picture --
with its overlay rectangles and its snapshot kept -- never a wait, so a busy
GPU costs a picture and not a cartridge frame.

The window (platform/window.c) is an SDL_WINDOW_VULKAN window and hands the
backend SDL's loader, the surface's extensions and the surface call as
function pointers; a host without a Vulkan window or device plays through the
surface path with the CPU scene renderer and one printed reason, and a run
that named `vk` keeps Vulkan through a readback (platform/vk_context.c). A
state a GL run wrote is read as `vk` with one line saying so.

Tests: present-vk-test replaces present-gl-test and adds D152's empty-list
rule, a skipped present's debts and D145's HUD frame over the wide world with
the blend; present-vk-window-test holds the swapchain across a resize and
fullscreen; test_shell.c's GL case is a host-backed backend's layout hook
instead (the assertion's subject changed with the backend, not its claim);
test_present_gpu.sh's window comparison runs 120 frames with two retries
instead of one frame, because the cold start's first frame is late and both
paths dropped it, comparing two undrawn targets (the shell's target is now
zeroed, so such a run is black rather than whatever malloc returned);
test_renderer_default.sh names `vk` and the new display-settings sentence.

Makefile: mk/vulkan.mk archives output/render_scene_vk*.c and both shaders,
mk/tests_host.mk gains present-vk-test and present-vk-window-test and loses the
two GL targets, mk/app.mk compiles app/play_presenter.c with MDP_HAVE_VK.
