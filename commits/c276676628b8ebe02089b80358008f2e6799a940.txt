ticket 217: the sound's events, the voices and the stop
The owner's second playtest: the voices were missing, the one sample that
played stopped the track, the effects and the music did not mix, and some notes
in the mission were not the cartridge's. The measurement is the ticket's first
deliverable: the sound API's ids, the resolved set/song, the channel and the
note, per event, on both paths from one recording.

* scripts/compare_sound_events.py and `headless --sound-log`: the sound API's
  play/stop command, the engine's play/stop/note/gate events and the Z80
  driver's destination pool, one line each. The tool runs the transliterated
  path (the C over the Z80 driver) and the port's engine over the same
  recording, prints the event table and applies the WAV route floor. The
  playtest route posts the same 15 commands and the mission the same 11; the
  paired notes agree on channel and number within the frame boundary.
* the kit gate is $16EB, which changes the sample player's mode; it is not a
  stop. The engine's gate wrote $2B=0 and killed the DAC after three frames, so
  the side-choice voice, the unit lines and the attack lines were silent. The
  voice window of the playtest route moves from -37.5 dB to -15.1 dB against
  the driver's -14.6 dB, and the sample now lasts its record.
* next_audio_engine_stop cleared every sequence channel, so PlayMusic's
  stop-then-play pair wiped the track it had just started. It now clears only
  the matching song's channels and calls the pool pass, as $0C77/$0CD2 do. On
  the battle route the stop at 7058 leaves 186 notes of the new track after it
  (0 before).
* the drum table's size is in its first sample offset: 31 records in the front
  end's set and 5 in the mission's, not the extractor's 16. The front end's
  voices 16-30 were filed as sample bytes and the mission's sample bytes as
  records; 449 files now round-trip.
* analysis/fixtures/audio/polyphony.events: music + two effects + the voice,
  four overlapping commands, 90 notes, compared event for event.

tests/test_sound_events.sh runs the A/B on the front end and the mission, the
polyphony fixture, the voice length, the stop's notes and the negative control;
docs/next-engine.md, docs/formats-music.md and docs/build-and-test.md carry the
tables and the before/after.
