180: the game's rules on a page, each one cited or measured
The game's rules are now a page (docs/game-rules.md): visibility, damage and
destruction, credits and spice, a structure's life, a mission's end, and
selection and orders -- 33 rules, each with the instruction that implements it,
the measurement that established it, or the observation that would settle it.

The measurements are new and committed: scripts/game_rules.py replays the two
committed mission recordings to eight named VBlank entries and keeps the 64 KiB
of work RAM (analysis/rules/), then re-derives the page's numeric claims from
the dumps with no emulator and no recording. The battle route settles what the
code alone could not: the explored bit is monotone (62 -> 92 cells), the
player's side knows 2 of 23 enemy units, a death frees an index that is
re-issued, a refinery pays 700 credits for a 100-unit spice load, and the end
gate is re-armed 300 mission ticks ahead.

Ten entry points the rules start at are named (IsPositionOnScreen,
RevealCellEdges, ReleaseDeadUnit, RunStructurePass, RunRefineryConversion,
CheckMissionLost, CheckMissionWon, UpdateMissionClockDigits,
OrderActionHarvest, OrderActionDeliverSpice), and four earlier readings are
corrected: the 46-word order budget is the drawn window, not visibility; the
void-tile mask counts unexplored neighbours; a record's map sprite is a VDP
slot; and the mission's end conditions are per-bit predicates whose counters
are only now identified.

The site renders the page, and every routine it cites links its rules back.
Two checks: tests/test_game_rules.py (fast: the page's structure, the measured
claims, and six negative controls for the citation gate) and
tests/test_game_rules_capture.sh (slow: the dumps regenerate byte for byte).
