Merge ticket 57: dump the reference's VDP state from the 68000 itself
The GDB stub's byte accesses could not read the control port correctly (ticket
52 documented why: the low byte first, and a pending first word turns the next
write into a command word). This ticket has the guest do it instead: at the
VBlank handler entry a 168-byte routine is uploaded to work RAM, the stacked PC
is patched so the handler's own rte enters it, and the routine reads
V-RAM/C-RAM/V-SRAM through $C00000 with a fresh address command before every
word. capture_vdp_state.py writes the dump next to the existing captures and
cross-checks it against the event log's replayed memories; compare_vdp_state.py
reports the first differing byte per structure with its meaning.

Verified: at frame 10, where the rendered frame is already pixel-identical,
V-RAM (65 536 bytes), C-RAM (128) and V-SRAM (80) all compare 0 differ and 23
of 24 registers agree - the exception is the write-only DMA length counter,
which the hardware decrements during a transfer and a write-event replay cannot
show (documented, and --strict-registers counts it). Mutated dumps are rejected
at the changed offset. The port dumps at the handler *exit* to meet the
reference's boundary (--vdp-dump-at exit). tests/run_all.sh: 19 PASS, ALL
CHECKS PASSED; cartridge byte-exact.
