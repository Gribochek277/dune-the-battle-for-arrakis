D154: what the player saw is measured -- a picture's refresh, its frame on screen, its motion
The window counted presents and schedule slots, and neither is a picture on
screen: a present that returns at once is queued, one that waits for the
display returns after it showed the picture, and a slot that went by without a
present call says nothing about either. The measurement is now the display's:

* app/present_log.{c,h}: every picture's hand-over and return, what was drawn
  (the pair's newest frame, the alpha, a cut) and the display's grid, through a
  FIFO model -- on screen from the first vblank after its hand-over and never
  in its predecessor's refresh. The totals: how many refreshes each picture
  stayed up, the cartridge frame nearest each picture refresh by refresh
  (repeated, skipped) and the content step's error against the display's
  clock. Arithmetic over the times the loop hands in, so it measures the GL
  swap, a Vulkan queue and SDL's blit alike.
* platform/window.c: mdp_window_refresh(), the display's period and a vblank
  from Windows' compositor (dwmapi.dll at run time, primary display, SDL's
  windows driver only); 0 everywhere else. The app reaches it through
  mda_platform.refresh.
* --report-present PATH writes one line per picture; the exit line's
  'presentation:' totals are printed with or without it.

The loop itself is unchanged here, so the same instrument measures the before.
Its first reading, the title at 2560x1440 fullscreen on the GL presenter: 898
pictures over 902 refreshes of 59.9999 Hz, 33 cartridge frames repeated and 30
skipped on screen, content steps off by up to 216/256 of a frame.

test_present.c: the model's fixtures (a picture per refresh, a late one, a
burst, the beat, a jittering clock, a cut). test_presentation.sh: every run
writes the report and it must agree with the totals; a control drops a line.
