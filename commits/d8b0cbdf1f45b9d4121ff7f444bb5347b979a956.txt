tests: the overlay's transform takes the viewport's first line (D156)
Since D156 the world region's source is the set of lines the viewport shows.
The source row is -(height - 240) / 2:

* 1920x1200 at 1x starts 480 lines above the cartridge's screen;
* 1300x600 at 3x and 4x starts 20 and 45 lines into it.

The check's transform assumed source row 0, so the owner case reported its
box 480 rows "off". The transform now takes the row off, read from the
run's own layout line.

At 1300x600 the viewport cuts line 225, the box's bottom corner. The overlay
drops a shape whose corner the picture does not cover, so the run reports
the box outside the picture and draws no stroke. The markers and the badge
whose corners are cut are absent too. Before D156 the source was the
cartridge's 240 lines whatever the target cut, and the box was drawn with
its bottom side off the target. The assertion is edited to state the
overlay's rule at those two cases. Every other case keeps its box, markers
and badge checks.
