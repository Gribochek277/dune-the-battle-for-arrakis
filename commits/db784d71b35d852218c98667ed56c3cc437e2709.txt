The widescreen shell: the window is the target, and the 4:3 picture keeps its shape
Ticket 120. The v2 renderer no longer draws into the VDP's 320x240: the playable
window's drawable pixels are the target, a layout says where the guest's picture
goes in it, and the 4:3 picture is integer-scaled, centred and pillarboxed or
letterboxed -- never stretched.

The layout (output/layout.{c,h}) is integer arithmetic: a target, regions (a
source rectangle in guest pixels, a scale, an anchor and margins), the
fit-height and wide-world modes, the integer scale policy, the separate
interface and world scales, and the view transform the mouse and the wide view
will share. The shell (output/shell.{c,h}) owns the layout, the target-sized
surface and the scene renderer's target, and reflows all three on a resize; it
refuses a renderer that is not the v2 one, so the shell is v2-only by
construction. md_scene gained guest_scale and the CPU backend expands each guest
pixel into a scale x scale block, caching the rendered guest row across the
target rows that show it. The window gained a target-sized texture path, a
resize and fullscreen (F11 and --fullscreen); the app loop takes the caller's
surface when there is a shell and its own 320x240 frame otherwise, so the
harness, the reference window and the fidelity path are untouched.

--aspect W:H (4:3, 16:9, 21:9), --window WxH, --fullscreen, --interface-scale N
and --world-scale N are the front end's; on any other renderer they are refused
with the sentence naming --renderer scene. A 4:3 scene run is the old 640x480
window at scale 2 with no bars.

Checks: port/build/layout-test (933 checks: the scale policy, anchors, both
modes, the transform round trip over nine targets and scales 1..3, a stale
destination that must fail, and the shell's target drawn through
--renderer scene), port/build/window-test (12, SDL's dummy driver), the scene
fixtures' integer-scale and scaled-blend cases, tests/test_layout.sh (fast) and
tests/test_shell.sh (slow: each target's report line, the v2-only refusal, and
the VDP dump identical across the reference, 4:3 and 16:9 runs). Fidelity's
nine checkpoints stay pixel-exact and the scene checkpoints stay identical.

Decision: docs/decisions.md D43. Prose: docs/widescreen.md,
docs/port-architecture.md and docs/native-port.md ("The widescreen shell").
