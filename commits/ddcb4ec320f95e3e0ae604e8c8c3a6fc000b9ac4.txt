The mouse: a backend, a map of two actions, and a pointer at the display's rate
Ticket 123. The mouse joins the keyboard and the gamepad as a third device in
the same shape -- a backend that reports its own controls, a map that says what
each one means -- plus the one thing a pad never needed: a pointer.

- platform/mouse.{c,h}: the position in window points and the five buttons, held
  and pressed, with MDP_MOUSE_TEST_SCRIPT for a scripted pointer (a line's state
  holds from its poll, so a test can wait for a screen).
- app/input_map.c: mda_input_mouse_map() binds the left button to a pointer
  click and the right to back. The shipped map binds no pad button, so a click
  cannot reach the guest as a pad button by accident; a map that binds one is
  the explicit choice and unions with the keyboard at the first port.
- app/pointer.{c,h} + md_layout_point_to_pixel(): the chain from a window point
  through the target's point grid (the --scale/HiDPI rule) and the shell's
  picture region to a guest pixel and back, and the arrow drawn into the
  finished surface. A bar belongs to no region, so a click there does nothing.
- The overlay is drawn after the renderer and before the present, once per
  picture, and reads the live position: the pointer moves at the display's rate
  while the guest keeps 59.92 frames a second (ticket 119's rule).
- next/pointer_state.{c,h} and one hook in each of the four screens: the title
  menu (the sprite record's Y), the port settings screen (state.cursor), the
  options screen ($FFFFBED6/$FFFFBECC) and the house selection (d4 and the
  strip's record). A move selects; an edge is latched until a screen takes it;
  each screen's setup forgets a click made on the screen before it.
- The row bands are now in the active area's cells (title rows 20..23, settings
  8, 10, ..., options 5, 7, ..., house 13/25), measured by patching each label
  and reading where the pixel moved; next_pointer_cell() is the one conversion.
- --mouse / --no-mouse, and --screenshot writes the last presented picture.
- tests: the pointer fixtures in layout-test (every scale/bar/fullscreen and HiDPI
  grid, the arrow, the cell), the mouse map's rows in input-map-test, and
  tests/test_mouse.sh -- five scripted runs under SDL's dummy driver that open
  PORT SETTINGS from the title, open ENTER PASSWORD from the options screen,
  move the house selection's strip to ORDOS, and prove the --no-mouse and bar
  controls unmoved and the pad recording empty. The picture's digest is pinned
  and the PNG cached in analysis/reference.
- Docs: docs/mouse.md, the input-device recipe, the band table, the glossary and
  D48.
