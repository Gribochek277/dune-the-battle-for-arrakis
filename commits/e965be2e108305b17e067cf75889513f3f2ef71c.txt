Ticket 130 took the half of the map view that runs every frame. This takes the
rest: the repaint (`RedrawMapAfterSideChange` `$00295E`, `RedrawMapObjects`
`$002A96`, `RedrawMapEdge` `$002B0C`), the cursor and the overlay
(`InitMissionMapCursor` `$0025DC`, `InitMissionMapCursor2` `$0025F2`,
`CenterMapOnCell` `$00271C`, `UpdateCursorVisibility` `$00273C`,
`InitMissionOverlay` `$00278E`). The map view is sixteen rows of the
replacement table now, one implementation, five files under
`port/next/mission_map_view_*`, each a region of the guest's image with one case
per guest instruction charged at its own address.

The map's objects are read from the cells, not a record list. `RedrawMapObjects`
walks one row of `MapCells`, starts every cell at `$0C` and colours a structure
cell (object word bit 13) with `data_025322[side]` when the record's side is
`CurrentSide`; units are sprites and are not painted by this pass. The map holds
18 structure cells at 7 898/8 148/9 498 (4 of the current side 1, 14 of side 2)
and 37/33/33 unit cells, so both branches of the side comparison are in the
route's data. A unit's death is `ClearUnitFromMapCells` clearing the cell's unit
bit and index (`andi.w #$EF00,$2(a0)`) and calling `InitMissionMapCursor`; the C
has no second copy to leave the dead unit on the map. The Atreides route's units
move but none dies, so the fixture clears a cell exactly that way and compares
the repaint.

The cursor's visibility bit is measured and the M2-4 hypothesis is corrected.
`BuildSpriteTable` `$001360` skips a sprite node whose byte `+7` has bit 7 set
(`$001394`), so bit 7 is hidden. `InitMissionMapCursor` hides the node at
`$FFFFBF60` (which is `$FFFFBF5A + 6`, the node the selection path positions with
`move.l $A(a3),$FFFFBF5A`) and cancels its pending show; `InitMissionMapCursor2`
does the same for the map's node at `$FFFFBF18` (`MapScrollPosition + 6`).
`UpdateCursorVisibility` shows the cursor now, or arms bit 1 of `$FFFFBF0D` for
the edge scroll to consume at the end of a chunk crossing (`$00374C` clears the
bit and then the node's bit 7). Bit 1 is a *pending show*, not "hide the cursor";
the curated lines are corrected. The node flags move with the route: the cursor's
`+7` is `$A8` (hidden) at 7 898 and `$28` (drawn) at 8 148, the map's is `$40`
(drawn with the priority bit) throughout.

The two helpers share the single `rts` at `$002606`: both spans stop before it
and it stays the guest's own code, which is D41's third span shape.
