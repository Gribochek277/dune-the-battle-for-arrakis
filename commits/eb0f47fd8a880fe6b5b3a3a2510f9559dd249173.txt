ticket 100: correct two findings of my own that the next hour disproved
The registers did not undo the copies and the guest was not restoring register 4 or
$10. Both were one bug: a register write must be a single word, and a long leaves the
model with a pending command that eats the next command's first word. Fixed, all
seventeen registers match the options screen's exactly -- which is also why the
'four bits cannot address register 16' note was wrong; the model decodes five.

The remaining gap is the loader's coverage, and the ticket now says plainly that
pattern-matching the ROM against the dump is the wrong tool here, because repeated
blank tiles make ROM $053234's first 32 bytes 'match' V-RAM at $5A40, $5A42 and
$5A46. That cost a detour; the destinations are in the selector table
LoadScreenResource indexes, and reading them there is the next attempt.
