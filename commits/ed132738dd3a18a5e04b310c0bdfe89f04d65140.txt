port: the run asks before it ends, and the unit band claims its first routine (D162, D163)
Two changes in the port's own C, and the entry pages the docs branch rewrote.

**A quit asks first (D162).** Escape, a gamepad control a binding file puts on
`quit`, and the window's close box used to end the run on the press; one of them
is one key too far from an evening's game. `mda_app_config.on_quit` now lets the
front end take the request over and `on_held` keeps the machine stopped while it
answers. The question is the port's screen (`app/quit_prompt.{c,h}`): NO is the
answer it opens on, the four directions choose, Enter/Start/A answer, the pad's B
and the quit key go back, and the controls are the *resolved pad's press edges*
(`mda_input.pad_pressed`, new) so a binding file rearranges them and a key held
down when the question appears is not a press. It is `next`'s alone -- D32's gate
is the arrangement, not the binary -- and its glyphs are the cartridge's own,
read out of the image's two font blocks by `next_font_tile()`'s rule, with a
two-pixel air between letters. The words are the locale's (`quit.title/yes/no`).
The hold's schedules are moved past it (`mda_pacing_hold()`), so its frames are
neither caught up in a burst nor given up as lost time; its price is silence.

`port/tests/test_quit_prompt.c` (79 checks) holds the state and the picture and
writes `port/build/quit-prompt.png`; `tests/test_quit_prompt.sh` drives a real
window: Escape asks and Enter answers NO, the pictures stop for the length of the
question (measured from `--report-present`, with an untouched run as the control),
the close box asks, `legacy` ends at once, and the same binary with `--engine
next` asks.

**The unit band's first claimed routine (issue 6, D163).** `sub_01E208` is the
click's snap: the cursor's cell, its structure, its tile class, then the first
unit of the cell and its eight neighbours (the nine signed offsets at `$01E276`),
returned as the record's own cell. The route is the mission's with the cursor
moved up at 7702 and A pressed at 7708
(`analysis/recordings/unit-band-cell-occupant.pad`): the command pass `$016FF6`
takes its "nothing selected" arm at `$017690` and calls it on **frame 7 709** --
78 dispatches, 78 distinct states, 5 branches, 4 one-armed and reported. The row
is `next/unit_band_19.c` (the span `$01E208-$01E289`, generated by
`scripts/transcribe_group.py`), the table is 653 rows and 650 implementations, and
`analysis/oracle/sub_01E208-01E208.fixture` is that entry's state. The corpus
regenerates to exactly it (456 = 455 + one), the fixture replays against the C,
and the group's new pair on the row's own route (7 750 frames) is `legacy` byte
for byte -- 50 314 dispatches, identical picture, work RAM, steps, cycles and
YM2612 timeline. `tests/test_oracle.sh`'s `UNSLICED` list is the eleven that
follow, each with the state it still needs.
