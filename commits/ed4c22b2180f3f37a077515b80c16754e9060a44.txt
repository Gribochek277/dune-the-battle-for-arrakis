194: the manifest carries a palette and a destination for every asset
The ticket asks one entry per asset for its palette and where it goes in video
memory. The tile blocks, the palettes and the two compressed blocks had both;
the name tables, the fonts and the glyph map were missing one or the other. A
map now records the palette the resource that uploads it loads with it, the
fonts record their three V-RAM destinations ($9000 resource 14's loader, $A000
the text planes, $4400 the boot self-test's failure message), the glyph map
says it is read and not uploaded, and a facing entry says its cells draw the
chunk's tiles at the cells' own tile numbers.
