The mission's unit turn is a check, and --no-refresh is its control
tests/test_mission_turn.sh replays the Atreides recording to VBlank entry
7700 and reads record 33's angle triple out of the port's work-RAM dump --
the step, target and current angle at $FF1274/$FF1275/$FF1276 -- where
ticket 109 measured the two runs parting.  It requires step $F8, target $68
and a current angle inside the turn, and it requires the idle-turn action
$01B860 and AimUnitTurret $01D47C in the port's own --address-log, so a
triple that turned for another reason cannot pass.

The control is the same command with --no-refresh: the instruction path
without the console's DRAM refresh charge, i.e. the clock the port had before
this ticket.  It reproduces the old triple exactly (00/C0/C0), which is what
makes the check measure the fix.  The harness option is a diagnostic, like
--perturb and --frame-cycles.
