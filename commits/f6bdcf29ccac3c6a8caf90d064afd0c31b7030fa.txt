docs+ticket 100: the screen resource table, with both size units, before any loader
Written up because the two lists an entry points at measure size differently and a
loader that assumed one would fill the options screen and the next screen wrongly:
UploadGraphicBlocks takes 32-byte tiles (asl.w #4 for the DMA word count, asl.w #5 to
advance the destination) while the per-upload lists take bytes (lsr.w #1, because the
VDP wants words). Bit 15 is RLE in both; the window position follows the lists from a
per-selector table. Entry $0E is 722 tiles from $001032A0 plus 3 584 bytes from
$00053234, uncompressed -- the 16 KiB verbatim match says so.
