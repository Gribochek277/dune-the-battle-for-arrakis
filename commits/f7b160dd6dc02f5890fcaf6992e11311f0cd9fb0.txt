D147: the wide mission's vertical bounds are the lines the target shows
D145 fills the target with the world and centres a world taller than the target,
so at 2560x1080 30 picture lines are cut above and 30 below -- 21 and 23 of the
cartridge's 224-line screen. The cartridge's bounds did not know: held up on the
Atreides mission, the camera stopped at the map's top (512) with its first 21
lines off the target, and the cursor's record went on to the cartridge's 24,
putting 13 of its bracket's 32 lines off the screen; at 1120x480 the cursor was
half off the screen even mid-map. The owner's requirement is that the whole map
stays reachable and visible and the pad's cursor scrolls at the visible edge.

* app/wide_mission.c: mda_wide_mission_cut_lines() reads the cut lines from
  the built layout (a line shown only partly counts as cut); play_display keeps
  them beside the extra and prints a `mission cut lines:` line when there are
  some; run_play hands them to the mission once a frame from the same layout.
* next/mission_wide.c: the cut lines less the VDP's border (9 above, 7 below)
  are the screen lines hidden. next_mission_wide_apply() now moves six words
  from their captured bases: ticket 152's two, the camera's world-y bounds
  $FFFFE3CE/$FFFFE3D0 outwards by the hidden lines (the map's first and last
  lines are then the target's), and the record's y clamps $FFFFBF1C/$FFFFBF20
  inwards (24 lines from the *visible* edge), each relearned after the
  placement mode's per-frame rewrite and put back once when its move is 0.
  The clamps stop one line outside the pad's dead zone at a cut that deep.
* the transcriptions read the clamps where the cartridge had constants, with the
  reason next to each case: $18/$C8 at $003C22/$003C2A/$003C44/$003C4C and the
  mission handler's screen bottom $DF at $003366 and $003620.
