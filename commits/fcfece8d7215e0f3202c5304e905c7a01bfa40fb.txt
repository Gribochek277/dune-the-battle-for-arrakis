ticket 215: the gate is atomic, shared, parallel and scoped, and it is measured
The owner's request was that the gates be faster, and the strategy is decided by
numbers, in the order the seconds pay.

Atomic. The flag inventory's rules are properties of the committed record, not of
a route, so they are `tests/test_flag_inventory.py` (0.3 s, five negative
controls: an address in two groups, a complete group owing a delegation, a
disabled row, a group the stage record lacks, a routine with a group but the
guest's state) and `tests/test_flag_groups.sh` keeps the part a fixture cannot
prove. The claims that stay whole-run are named in docs/build-and-test.md with
the reason: a picture, a chip timeline, a cycle count, a whole-run regeneration
and "the played build finishes the route in a window".

Shared. `tests/lib/gate_shared.sh` is the route-head cache: the harness's
`--state-save` state at the frame a route's subtree begins (the Atreides
mission's first entry, 7 686 of 9 500), keyed by the hashes of the binary, the
ROM and the recording plus the selection and the frame. `tests/test_units.sh`
(the three mission checkpoints), `tests/test_mission_layers.sh`,
`tests/test_mission_credits.sh` and `tests/test_mission_turn.sh` start from one
and run the tail; the last two keep their controls whole because the clock those
runs accumulate is the control's subject. The limit is measured, not assumed:
the mission head is byte-identical at 8 148 and 9 498, the battle route's is 4
instructions and 2 cycles off at 8 600, so the units check keeps the battle
route's banked states whole. `tests/test_shared_state.sh` proves the identity on
a constructed route and the controls (another selection is another key, a
damaged head is refused, `--shared off` builds its own). `tests/prepare.sh`
builds every port target more than one check names, once (6 min 08 s cold
instead of one build per check).

Parallel. `tests/run_all.sh` schedules the slow tier in three groups: `run_slow`
(the harness-only checks) at `--jobs` (12 on the 16-core host), `run_x` (the
windowed-emulator and real-window checks) at 3, and `run_paced`/`run_build` alone
after them (the wall-clock/audio measurements and the control builds that relink
the binary every other check runs). Two concurrency defects the first parallel
run found, fixed: `tests/gate-bin/make` serializes the checks' makes (they raced
on build/build_identity.h.tmp), and the private X display range is :90-:129.

Scoped. `tests/run_all.sh --scope [BASE]` runs the checks `scripts/gate_scope.py`
selects from the diff -- the runner's own commands, the port's groups and an area
table -- and `tests/test_gate_scope.py` holds the mapping to its claims (every
check reachable, every group mapped, a targeted change targeted, a docs change
not selecting a picture gate, an unclassified path selecting the fast tier).
`bash tests/run_all.sh` with no argument is still the whole default tier.

Measured on this machine (docs/build-and-test.md, "The gate's tiers", with every
check's seconds rendered by scripts/gate_table.py from the run's own timings):
the default tier 48 min 19 s -> 22 min 15 s, the slow tier about 3 h 19 min ->
2 h 14 min, `--tier all` about 4 h 07 min -> 2 h 36 min; the slow tier's 343 min
of check time ran in 134 min. The host was shared with another worker's gate for
the after runs, so those seconds are upper bounds and the page says so.

The slow tier also had failures that predate this ticket, and the run found them:
tests/test_mission_edge_band.sh and test_mission_pointer_scroll.sh built `play`
and copied `dune-port-compare` (since ticket 199 split the binaries), the edge
band's control returned 0 where the mechanism now reads "the C did not run" and
faults, scripts/game_rules.py read the work RAM from the end of the machine
section that ticket 198's battery moved, tests/test_frame_clock.sh required the
table's only interrupt-entered row after ticket 216 added a second, and
tests/test_gamepad.py drove the played build with `--engine legacy`, which that
build refuses. Each is fixed here with the reason beside it.
