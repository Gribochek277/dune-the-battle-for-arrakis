port: fix the four emitter defects, with dual-path fixtures
Ticket 44. Four defects were recorded in docs/native-port.md and deliberately
left unpatched until each had a test; this fixes them and adds the fixture
mechanism that makes "fails before, passes after" reproducible.

The fixtures
- port/tests/fixtures/fixtures.asm is the single source of truth: the Makefile
  assembles it into build/fixtures.bin (copied into work RAM) and runs
  scripts/transpile68k.py over the same file into build/fixtures.c, so the
  bytes the test executes and the emitters it exercises cannot drift.
- md68k-fixture-test links that generated translation as md68k_translated_step
  (the game's name, in a binary that does not link game.c), so
  md68k_set_translation() really selects between the generated cases and the
  interpreter. Each fixture runs twice and the runs must agree on every
  register, SR and all of work RAM, on top of the PRM expectation the fixture
  asserts; a translated run that falls back to the interpreter fails the
  fixture.
- transpile68k.py gains --rom-base (read extension words from an image at its
  own origin) and --function (generated entry-point name); the branch and
  write-EA operand readers now go through the same base-aware helpers as the
  rest of the decoder.

The fixes
- md68k_set_nz() takes the operand size and masks inside it; it is now the only
  N/Z helper (md68k_set_nz_sized() is folded into it), and move/moveq/tst pass
  the size, so byte and word decisions cannot see stale high bits.
- clr and tst clear V and C, as the 68000 does.
- the DBcc emitter follows the manual: condition true leaves the counter alone
  and falls through; otherwise the low word is decremented and the branch is
  taken while the result is not -1. The displacement is relative to the
  extension word. The whole DBcc family dispatches through the emitter, so dbf
  (273 uses in the ROM) is translated too.
- lea/pea/jsr/jmp share Emitter.ea_address(), the transpiler counterpart of the
  interpreter's ea_address(), instead of hand-built operands; pea computes its
  address before A7 moves, and jsr/jmp sign-extend absolute-short targets. The
  interpreter now computes a JSR destination before pushing, so both paths
  target the old A7 for jsr (a7) (PRM operation order; Musashi and BlastEm
  agree).

Also fixed: add/sub/and/or with a Dn destination assigned the size-masked
result to the whole register, zeroing the high bits of a byte or word
operation. The new fixtures found that one on their first run.
