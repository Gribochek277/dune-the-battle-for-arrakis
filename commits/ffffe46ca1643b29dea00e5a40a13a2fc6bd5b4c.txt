ticket 100: the resource table, measured as far as it is measured
Entry $0E is two eight-byte descriptors -- {$0000 $0E00 $0005 $3234} and
{$0000 $02D2 $0010 $32A0} -- in a table of sixteen-byte entries at $02243A, each
long of an entry either zero or the address of one. Two sources $6C apart with
lengths $0005 and $0010 is the shape of a palette and its graphics next to each
other in one bank.

Which bank is a lead and is written as one: of the candidate bases tried, $030000 is
the only one where those offsets land on bytes that look like 4bpp tile planes
instead of zeros or code. The ticket says what settles it -- copy the length the
descriptor gives to the destination the descriptor gives, and compare the VDP dump
with the options screen's own at entry 3000 of menu-options.pad -- and what a
failure would mean, because ruling out a bank is worth an hour either way.

Not written up as a section of docs/game-architecture.md yet. The ticket's criterion
is that the format be known before the loader is written, and half-known is the
state that produces a loader which works on the options screen and quietly does not
on the next one.
